# Ship PCN closures as facts, reviews as hints

For an offline Singapore cycling overlay, the most reliable data is official NParks, PCN, Rail Corridor and NEA notices about closures and no-cycling rules. Crowd sentiment is the weakest layer. No researcher could retrieve Reddit, HardwareZone, Strava or Google Maps review threads, so almost every "good" or "mixed" label in this report comes from editorial blogs, listicles and a handful of Trip.com, Wanderlog and Tripadvisor snippets. No source called any PCN stop "overrated". The negatives are about **crowds, dismount rules, missing water and closures**, not about how good a place is. As of 20 Nov 2026 the network carries a large set of dated closures. The biggest are **Punggol Point Park (closed 11 Aug 2026 to about 31 Dec 2027, toilets still open)**, **Rail Corridor South from Henderson Rd to Spooner Rd (closed to 31 Jul 2027)**, the **Loyang PC (to Q3 2029)**, the **AMK Ave 6 PC on the C2C (to end 2028)**, the **Kallang PC near St Michael's (to 2H 2027)**, the **Alexandra–Queensway PC (to Q3 2027)** and the **Gardens by the Bay Waterfront Promenade (closed to cyclists since 4 May 2026, no end date)**. Several food anchors also close or have closed: Jumbo at East Coast Seafood Centre, Telok Blangah Rise MFC, Bukit Timah MFC and Bollywood Farms. Rest stops are densest on the east and north-east (ECP, Pasir Ris, Punggol, Sengkang). The thinnest stretches are Changi Coastal (a toilet with no water), the Rail Corridor (no on-trail toilets), Kranji/Lim Chu Kang, and the Ulu Sembawang/Mandai side of the Northern Explorer Loop. Only two coordinates in the whole dataset come from a source. Every other lat/lon must be geocoded before shipping.

## How to read the tables

Each per-loop table maps directly to a JSON record. **Sentiment** takes one of five values. `good` means positive editorial or review evidence. `mixed` means the place is liked but has a stated practical drawback, and the reason column carries that drawback, so it can become a reason code such as crowded, dismount, no-water or limited-food. `bad` means a stated problem such as a hazard, no water or a dirty facility. `unrated` means no review was found. `n/a` applies to closures and rules. **Status** is as of 20 Nov 2026 and includes an end date wherever one was published. **Lat/lon** is blank unless known. Two values, marked *(src)*, come from NParks "Navigate here" links. Values marked `~` are approximate landmark centroids from one researcher's general knowledge, accurate to about 100 m. They carry **no source** and must be checked against OneMap or OSM. Everything else should be geocoded from the official address or from the NParks PCN dataset on [data.gov.sg](https://data.gov.sg/collections/1490/datasets/d_a69ef89737379f231d2ae93fd1c5707f/view).

A loop-level caveat applies throughout. MND has said toilets exist only at **selected PCN sites**, and it directs riders to malls and food centres instead ([MND](https://mnd.gov.sg/newsroom/speeches/view/written-answer-by-ministry-of-national-development-on-toilets-at-park-connectors)). Water coolers are scarce even on popular routes ([The Star, 2023](https://thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2023/05/20/beating-the-hot-weather-with-water)). Riders on the 75 km Round Island Route plan water stops at petrol stations and coffeeshops roughly every 10–20 km ([Bikemap](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/8131159)), and guides warn that "there aren't many rest stops along the way" ([TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/round-island-route/)). A sensible app rule is to offer a stop every 10–15 km on 20+ km rides, never leave a gap of more than about 20 km, and rank stops by water plus toilet plus shade first, then food, then bike parking. That rule is an inference from route-planner behaviour, not a stated rider consensus.

The 25 km loop has six MRT stations on it, and "drinks and snacks are easily available in the housing estates along the route" ([CNA 2010](https://wildsingaporenews.blogspot.com/2010/11/northern-explorer-pcn-links-up-11-parks.html)). So HDB coffeeshops are a legitimate fallback even where no park facility is mapped. The practical chain runs Lower Seletar / Rower's Bay, then Yishun Park HC, Sembawang Park, and Admiralty / Woodlands North Coast. The Ulu Sembawang–Mandai side, which includes the slope, has the fewest facilities. These spacings are estimated, not sourced. The NParks brochure map marks restrooms and water points, but the extracted text does not say where ([NParks NEL brochure](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/PCN%20NEL%20Brochure.pdf)). Its 2012 date also predates Rower's Bay. Sembawang beach is a real data conflict. The NParks page still carries an NEA advisory, while a June 2026 grading seen only in a search snippet rated it "Good". ## Only official notices are safe to ship as hard flags

The data falls into three trust tiers. The JSON should carry the tier explicitly, so the app never presents a blog's opinion with the same weight as a gazetted rule.

| Field | Trust | Ship as | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `no_cycling` (reserves, boardwalks, Henderson Waves, Canopy Walk, JLG zones, GBTB interior) | High | **Hard flag**, routing constraint | Standing NParks / GBTB rules with no expiry ([NParks](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)) |
| `closure` with a published start and end date (PCN notices, Rail Corridor notices, NParks park signs, NEA hawker list) | High | **Hard flag** with `valid_until` | Primary sources with dates; they expire, so the app must know when |
| `closure` with an open end (GBTB promenade, AMK Town Garden West, RC North diversion, Telok Blangah trails) | Medium | Hard flag rendered as "closed — status may have changed" | Official start, but no confirmed reopening |
| `permanently_closed` from multiple news sources (Wheeler's Yard, Jumbo ECSC, Big Splash) | High | Exclude from the POI list, or keep as a tombstone | Corroborated |
| `reported_closed` from a single source (1932 Story Cafe, Soek Seng 1954) | Low–medium | Soft: "reported closed" | One source, undated or 2025 |
| Amenities (toilet, water, repair station, showers) | Medium–low | Soft hint with `as_of` year | Mostly from 2019–2023 blogs; Lorong Halus water and Coney toilets conflict |
| `sentiment` + reason code | Low | **Soft hint only**, never used to filter routes | Editorial; no crowd-review aggregates retrieved |
| Crowding | Medium | Time-scoped hint (weekend / public-holiday mornings) | Consistent across sources, but conditional |
| `lat/lon` | Very low | **Do not ship** the `~` values; geocode from OneMap / data.gov.sg | Only 2 of more than 100 points are sourced |

Several conflicts in the notes need a decision before release. **Punggol Point Park** is listed as open and good in one note and closed to end-2027 in two others; the dated NParks sign wins. **Rail Corridor South Phase 2** starts on 2 Mar or 9 Mar 2026, but it ends 31 Jul 2027 either way. The **Long Island** works start at "end 2026" or "progressively from Jan 2027", depending on the URA release, and paths stay open in both versions ([URA pr26-50](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-50/); [URA pr26-79](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-79/)). **Changi Village HC** closed 1–15 Oct or 5–9 Oct, both now past, and a Dec 2027 closure is unconfirmed. The **Sembawang beach** grading is "Fair" (Jan 2025) or "Good" (a June 2026 snippet). **Lorong Halus** has a water cooler in one note and none in another. **Jumbo** vacated on 30 Sep or in October. **Clementi 448** reopened on 28 or 29 Jun. None of these changes a hard flag on 20 Nov 2026 except Punggol Point.

## Conclusion

The offline constraint matters more than the review gap. A connected app can re-check a closure. This app will carry every flag past its end date. Every temporary status therefore needs a `valid_until`. When that date passes, the app should degrade the flag to "check status" rather than silently flip it to "open". Several of the 2026 closures (Bedok PC, Telok Blangah Rise, the Punggol Container Park) lapse within six weeks of this report, and others run to 2027–2029, so the overlay's shelf life is set by its nearest expiry. The time-limited NEA hawker cleaning windows are better shipped as a bundled schedule ([NEA list](https://www.nea.gov.sg/docs/default-source/hawker-centres-documents/list-of-hcs_-17-august-2026.pdf)) than as one-off flags.

The more useful product insight is that "mixed" almost never means a place is bad. It means crowded at a predictable time, a dismount point, or no water. Encoding sentiment as a reason code tied to time and amenity turns thin editorial evidence into actionable routing hints, such as "fill up before Changi Coastal" or "avoid ECP Area E at weekend mid-morning", without overclaiming quality judgements the data cannot support. The next data pass should go to the NParks PCN and amenity GeoJSON on data.gov.sg for coordinates, toilets and water points. Crowd reviews can wait. Geocoding, and the water gaps at Changi Coastal, the Rail Corridor and Kranji, are what will actually strand riders on a 20+ km ride.
