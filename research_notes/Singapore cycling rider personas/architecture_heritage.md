# Architecture Lover & Heritage/History Rider Personas: Singapore PCN, Rail Corridor, Coast-to-Coast, Round Island Route

Research date: 2026-11-20. Sources are dated where possible; blog route reports from 2021–2022 are flagged. **No coordinates are given anywhere in these notes.** None of the fetched pages gave coordinates I could check, and the brief says not to invent them. App builders should geocode from an authoritative dataset such as OneMap or the data.gov.sg PCN loop dataset ([data.gov.sg PCN loop](https://data.gov.sg/dataset/park-connector-loop)).

## Which architecture and heritage spots are realistically reachable by bike from the PCN/Rail Corridor?

### Takeaway
The best clusters with sources are: (1) the Rail Corridor, with the Bukit Timah Railway Station node, the Bukit Timah Truss Bridge, the former Tanjong Pagar station at its southern end, and the Alexandra–Queensway crossing; (2) the Marina Bay / Kallang Basin loop, with Helix, Jubilee, Merdeka and Benjamin Sheares bridges, Esplanade, One Fullerton, MBS/ArtScience and the Gardens by the Bay conservatories; (3) the Southern Ridges, with Henderson Waves and Alexandra Arch (likely push-bike), the Labrador WWII relics and Reflections at Bukit Chandu; (4) the Coast-to-Coast spine, with the Old Bukit Timah Fire Station, the Former Ford Factory area and the Truss Bridge; (5) the RIR east, with Seletar/Jalan Kayu RAF heritage and Kranji in the north. Both personas want slow, stop-heavy rides with places to park bikes and good photo angles. Heritage riders also want story context: NHB markers, galleries, and dress or etiquette guidance.

### Persona needs (inferred from the sources below, not from a survey)
- **Architecture lover:** wants skyline and bridge views from the saddle; golden-hour and night lighting (Marina Bay bridges); short detours (under 300 m) to iconic buildings; photo stops where the bike can be parked; flat, continuous routes such as Marina Bay and Kallang Basin, where landmarks come one after another ([Little Day Out, Jul 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)).
- **Heritage rider:** wants a story line (WWII, railway, kampong, colonial); NHB trail markers and galleries; opening hours and dress codes up front; respectful behaviour at living sites such as Kampong Lorong Buangkok and places of worship; routes that follow a heritage trail, such as the Rail Corridor or the NHB Woodlands trail ([NHB Woodlands trail release, 2022](https://www.nhb.gov.sg/-/media/nhb/files/media/releases/2022/media-release---head-up-north-to-get-the-lowdown-on-woodlands-with-nhb_s-latest-heritage-trail.ashx)).

### Cited Findings — candidate spots

**A. Architecture (bridges and modern buildings)**
1. **Helix Bridge** (Marina Bay). DNA-inspired curved pedestrian bridge and a popular lookout. Listed by Roots.gov.sg as a Jubilee Walk landmark. The Marina Bay loop rides *under* it, not over it. Nearest route: Marina Bay / Kallang Basin loop. — [Roots.gov.sg Helix Bridge](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/places/landmarks/jubilee-walk/helix-bridge); [Little Day Out Helix story](https://www.littledayout.com/little-stories-helix-bridge-inspired-by-dna/); [Little Day Out route, 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/); [Wikipedia: carries pedestrians](https://www.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Helix_Bridge)
2. **Jubilee Bridge** (Esplanade to Merlion Park). Designed by Arup for pedestrian *and cyclist* movement around Marina Bay. The 2021 route report rode across it with no dismount noted. — [Arup](https://www.arup.com/projects/jubilee-bridge-singapore); [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/singapore/jubilee-bridge-in-singapore); [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)
3. **Esplanade – Theatres on the Bay**, **One Fullerton** and **Merlion Park**. All are passed directly on the Marina Bay loop. — [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)
4. **Marina Bay Sands / ArtScience Museum** bayfront. The loop runs along the bayfront in front of them. — [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)
5. **Gardens by the Bay Cooled Conservatories** and **Marina Barrage**. Both are on the loop. — [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)
6. **Merdeka Bridge** (viewing spot) and **Benjamin Sheares Bridge** (ridden under). Kallang Basin segment. — [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)
7. **Singapore Sports Hub / Tanjong Rhu suspension bridge**. Kallang Basin segment. The suspension bridge is dismount-and-push. — [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)
8. **Henderson Waves** (Southern Ridges). Wave-form timber-ribbed bridge. Wikipedia lists it as carrying pedestrians and bicycles, but a guide says Henderson Waves and Forest Walk are pedestrian-only. Treat it as **push-bike**. Nearest route: Southern Ridges, linked to the Alexandra Park Connector and the RIR towards Labrador. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henderson_Waves); [Singapore Spirit guide](https://www.singapore-spirit.com/guides/southern-ridges-henderson-waves/); [Wikipedia Southern Ridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Ridges)
9. **The Pinnacle@Duxton**. Seven linked 50-storey public housing towers, about 156 m tall, with sky gardens. Its landscaping extends Duxton Plain Park, which a TheSmartLocal trail describes as rideable through conserved shophouse backlanes on the Botanic Gardens to Marina Bay trail. — [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/5558222); [Wikipedia mirror](https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/The_Pinnacle%40Duxton); [TheSmartLocal Botanic Gardens–Marina Bay trail](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/botanic-gardens-marina-bay-trail/)
10. **The Interlace** (Alexandra/Depot Road). The only link found is EdgeProp listing the Alexandra–Queensway park connector among its nearby trails. Proximity is plausible but not measured. — [EdgeProp](https://edgeprop.sg/property-news/city-life-amid-greenery-and-close-knit-community)
11. **Sengkang Bridge** (Sengkang Riverside Park). Described as the "largest bridge across a canal": 290 m span, 5.5 m wide. On the RIR east. — [Little Day Out, Jan 2022](https://www.littledayout.com/eastern-round-island-route-sights-landmarks-along-the-75-km-coastal-adventure/)
12. **Keppel Marina East Desalination Plant green roof**. Publicly accessible green roof on the RIR / East Coast stretch. — [Little Day Out, Jan 2022](https://www.littledayout.com/eastern-round-island-route-sights-landmarks-along-the-75-km-coastal-adventure/)

**B. Heritage (railway, WWII, colonial, kampong, religious)**
13. **Bukit Timah Railway Station** (1932) and **Railway Staff Quarters**. Conserved buildings on a 4.3 ha community node at the Rail Corridor midpoint, reopened 1 July 2022. The station is now a heritage gallery and the quarters are the 1932 Story Café. No carpark; wheelchair-accessible. Nearest route: Rail Corridor (Central). — [NParks Rail Corridor page](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/bukit-timah-railway-station/); [NParks news, 2022](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2022/6/new-bukit-timah-railway-station-community-node-welcomes-visitors--with-restored-railway-heritage-features-and-lush-gardens); [URA 2021](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr21-05/)
14. **Bukit Timah Truss Bridge** and **Old Bukit Timah Fire Station**. Both are named landmarks on or next to the Coast-to-Coast trail near Bukit Timah Nature Reserve. — [Little Day Out C2C](https://www.littledayout.com/coast-to-coast-trail-explore-sights-and-nature-from-jurong-lake-gardens-to-coney-island-park/); [NParks C2C trail guide PDF, Dec 2024](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/C2C_Trail_Guide_181224.pdf)
15. **Former Ford Factory** (WWII surrender site). Shown on a C2C checkpoint map as nearby. Distance from the trail was not verified. — [Little Day Out C2C](https://www.littledayout.com/coast-to-coast-trail-explore-sights-and-nature-from-jurong-lake-gardens-to-coney-island-park/)
16. **Former Tanjong Pagar Railway Station**. Southern terminus of the old KTM line. Closed for redevelopment into a community space above Cantonment MRT (CCL6). Its platform canopies are to be reinstated. See closures. — [SLA 2016](https://sla.gov.sg/articles/press-releases/2016/train-platform-canopy-structures-of-former-tanjong-pagar-railway-station-to-be-fully-reinstated-after-completion-of-new-mrt-station); [The Star, Aug 2022](https://thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2022/08/29/tanjong-pagar-railway-station-to-undergo-restoration-completion-expected-by-2026); [LTA, May 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/5/news-releases/circle-line-stage-6-to-open-for-public-preview-on-4-july-2026.html)
17. **Labrador Nature Reserve WWII relics**. Machine-gun pillboxes (one by an old school playground, one behind Toilet B) and a relic of a Labrador Battery 6-inch gun. There are also pre-WWII fort remains from about 1886 (portcullis near Carpark B, the Casemate at the hilltop). At the RIR Phase 1 western end. — [NParks War Relics](https://nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/labrador-nature-reserve/special-features/war-relics); [NParks RIR Phase 1, 2022](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2022/1/nparks-completes-phase-one-of-the-round-island-route-with-75km-of-recreational-connection-stretching-across-eastern-half-of-singapore)
18. **Reflections at Bukit Chandu**. NHB / National Museum WWII interpretive centre on the Battle of Pasir Panjang and the Malay Regiment. Reopened September 2021 after closing in 2018. On the Southern Ridges (Kent Ridge side). — [NHB media release](https://www.nhb.gov.sg/bukitchandu/about-us/media-release); [Bakchormeeboy, Sep 2021](https://bakchormeeboy.com/2021/09/11/reflections-at-bukit-chandu-reopens-with-an-enhanced-retelling-of-the-battle-of-pasir-panjang-and-the-malay-regiment/)
19. **Kranji War Memorial / Cemetery** (9 Woodlands Road). Unveiled 1946, with more than 24,000 names of Allied personnel with no known grave. Part of the NHB Woodlands Heritage Trail. Reached on the Kranji–Lim Chu Kang ride from Kranji MRT, which sits on the planned western RIR alignment. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Kranji_War_Memorial); [Roots: Kranji's WWII story](https://roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/kranji-world-war-ii-story/story); [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-in-singapore/)
20. **Jalan Kayu former RAF Seletar Base guardhouse**. At the RIR Jalan Kayu node, via the Seletar West PCN, with toilets and a water point. **Seletar Aerospace heritage trees node** (one of Singapore's largest broad-leafed mahoganies) is beside The Oval. — [Little Day Out, Jan 2022](https://www.littledayout.com/eastern-round-island-route-sights-landmarks-along-the-75-km-coastal-adventure/)
21. **Jane Buyers tombstone** (Rower's Bay, a short detour off the RIR). — [Little Day Out, Jan 2022](https://www.littledayout.com/eastern-round-island-route-sights-landmarks-along-the-75-km-coastal-adventure/)
22. **Sultan Mosque** (Kampong Gelam). The national-monument mosque. Visitor hours, robes and rules are under etiquette below. Reached via city cycling paths; no PCN link was verified. — [sultanmosque.sg](https://sultanmosque.sg/visitors-info)
23. **Kampong Lorong Buangkok**. Mainland Singapore's last kampong, on private land with residents still living there. Guides recommend a trusted guide, no photos into homes, and staying off private property. Guides disagree on whether free walk-ins are acceptable. — [Seek Sophie](https://seeksophie.com/magazine/a/a-trip-to-singapores-last-kampong--lorong-buangkok); [Nomad eSIM guide](https://www.nomadesim.com/ko/destination-guides/kampong-lorong-buangkok-singapore)
24. **Duxton conserved shophouse backlanes**. Rideable via Duxton Plain Park. — [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/botanic-gardens-marina-bay-trail/)

**Route context**
- The Coast-to-Coast trail runs 36 km from Jurong Lake Gardens to Coney Island, through 18 parks and 11 PCNs. Checkpoint lists differ by source; use the NParks guide. — [Mothership 2019](https://mothership.sg/2019/03/36km-coast-to-coast-trail-singapore); [NParks C2C guide](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/C2C_Trail_Guide_181224.pdf)
- The RIR is planned at 150 km. Phase 1 (75 km, Seletar to Labrador) opened 22 January 2022. The Eastern Corridor was completed in February 2024. The rest is to be completed progressively by 2035. — [NParks 2022](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2022/1/nparks-completes-phase-one-of-the-round-island-route-with-75km-of-recreational-connection-stretching-across-eastern-half-of-singapore); [NParks 2024](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2024/2/completion-of-the-eastern-corridor)
- The RIR east route crosses over the Rail Corridor between the Alexandra–Queensway PCN, Queensway and Alexandra Road. — [Little Day Out, Jan 2022](https://www.littledayout.com/eastern-round-island-route-sights-landmarks-along-the-75-km-coastal-adventure/)

### Inferences
- The flattest and densest architecture ride is the Marina Bay / Kallang Basin loop, with about 8 landmarks in roughly 10–15 km. The best heritage spine is the Rail Corridor plus the C2C around Bukit Timah: railway station, truss bridge, fire station and Ford Factory.
- A combined "Southern Ridges WWII + bridges" ride (Labrador to Bukit Chandu via Henderson Waves and Alexandra Arch) fits both personas, but expect hills and push-bike sections.
- Kranji is reachable by bike from Kranji MRT, but the western RIR is not complete. The app should warn riders about mixed road and PCN sections there (inferred from the 2035 completion target).
- Sultan Mosque and Kampong Lorong Buangkok are "ride-to, then walk" destinations. They suit a short dismounted visit, not a pass-by.

### Gaps
- **Coordinates:** none sourced. Do not hard-code; geocode via OneMap.
- **300 m proximity is not verified** for: The Interlace, Former Ford Factory, Sultan Mosque, Kampong Lorong Buangkok, Pinnacle@Duxton (it connects to Duxton Plain Park, but not to a PCN as such) and Jewel Changi. **Jewel Changi Airport** and **Alexandra Arch** details (beyond Southern Ridges membership) were not fetched, so they are not cited above.
- **Iconic HDB estates** (Toa Payoh, Queenstown Dawson, Tiong Bahru) were not researched in depth. Roots lists the Toa Payoh trails "Of Public Housing & Shared Spaces" ([Roots](https://roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/trails/Toa%20Payoh%20Heritage%20Trail%20-%20%20Of%20Public%20Housing%20and%20Shared%20Spaces)), but PCN proximity was not checked.
- **Temples and churches** near connectors (for example Siong Lim / Lian Shan Shuang Lin near the Kallang PCN, Hougang churches) were not verified in this pass.

## Which have NHB heritage markers or official heritage trail status?

### Takeaway
NHB trails that overlap these routes include Woodlands (Kranji War Memorial, 15 sites and 8 markers), Bukit Timah, Civic District (Marina Bay / Esplanade side), Jalan Besar, Toa Payoh, Hougang, Changi, Jurong and Orchard. The Helix Bridge is listed by Roots.gov.sg as a Jubilee Walk landmark. Bukit Timah Railway Station is a gazetted conserved building, but that is a URA/SLA status, not an NHB trail.

### Cited Findings
- The NHB Woodlands Heritage Trail (launched 2022) has 15 heritage sites and 8 trail markers and covers the area's military history, including Kranji. — [NHB media release 2022](https://www.nhb.gov.sg/-/media/nhb/files/media/releases/2022/media-release---head-up-north-to-get-the-lowdown-on-woodlands-with-nhb_s-latest-heritage-trail.ashx); [Roots Woodlands trail](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/trails/Woodlands%20Heritage%20Trail)
- The Roots.gov.sg trails index lists Jalan Besar, Jurong, Orchard, Bedok, Changi, Toa Payoh, Bukit Timah, Hougang, Woodlands, Sentosa and Civic District heritage trails. — [Roots trails](https://www.roots.gov.sg/nhb/trails); [Bukit Timah HT](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/trails/Bukit-Timah-Heritage-Trail-Learning-and-Leisure); [Changi HT](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/trails/changi-heritage-trail); [Hougang HT](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/trails/hougang-heritage-trail---landmarks-of-hougang); [Civic District map](https://www.roots.gov.sg/-/media/Roots/Files/Civic-District-Heritage-Trail/Civic-District-Map.ashx)
- The Helix Bridge has a Roots.gov.sg landmark page under Jubilee Walk. — [Roots](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/places/landmarks/jubilee-walk/helix-bridge)
- Bukit Timah Railway Station was returned to Singapore in 2011 and gazetted as a conserved building. It now houses a heritage gallery. — [URA PR21-05](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr21-05/); [NParks 2022](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2022/6/new-bukit-timah-railway-station-community-node-welcomes-visitors--with-restored-railway-heritage-features-and-lush-gardens)
- Reflections at Bukit Chandu is run by the National Museum of Singapore under NHB. — [NHB Bukit Chandu](https://www.nhb.gov.sg/bukitchandu/about-us/media-release)

### Inferences
- An app could tag spots as "NHB trail" (Kranji via Woodlands, Civic District items, Bukit Timah items), "NHB museum" (Bukit Chandu), or "Conserved building, URA" (Bukit Timah station, Duxton shophouses).

### Gaps
- A Kampong Gelam or Queenstown NHB trail page did not appear in searches. Queenstown is generally known for one, but it is unconfirmed here.
- Individual marker locations along each trail were not extracted.

## Any closures/restrictions as of 2026? (incl. etiquette)

### Takeaway
Key 2026 items: the Rail Corridor (South) section between Henderson Road and Spooner Road has been closed since 9 March 2026 with no end date given. The former Tanjong Pagar station is still not open; LTA in May 2026 referred to it only as "when it is ready". The Labrador tunnels are tour-only. The western RIR is incomplete (target 2035). There are dismount points around Marina Bay. The Southern Ridges bridges are best treated as push-bike.

### Cited Findings
**Closures**
- Since 9 March 2026, the Rail Corridor (South) between the Henderson Road and Spooner Road access points is closed for Phase 2 Trail Improvement Works. No end date is given. — [NParks Rail Corridor](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/bukit-timah-railway-station/)
- Former Tanjong Pagar Railway Station: restoration was due to start in 2024 with completion "by 2026" (2022 report). CCL6 (Cantonment) opened 12 July 2026, and LTA describes the entrances as linking to the station "when it is ready". No reopening date was found. — [The Star 2022](https://thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2022/08/29/tanjong-pagar-railway-station-to-undergo-restoration-completion-expected-by-2026); [LTA May 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/5/news-releases/circle-line-stage-6-to-open-for-public-preview-on-4-july-2026.html); [Mothership Jun 2026](https://mothership.sg/2026/06/3-new-mrt-station/)
- Labrador WWII tunnels: closed to the public, accessible only on NParks tours. — [NParks War Relics](https://nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/labrador-nature-reserve/special-features/war-relics)
- RIR: only the eastern half (Phase 1, 2022) and the Eastern Corridor (2024) are confirmed complete. The rest is due by 2035. No 2026 segment update was found. — [NParks 2024](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2024/2/completion-of-the-eastern-corridor)
- Event closures: cycling was banned on the Helix and Jubilee bridges and around Marina Bay on New Year's Eve 2024. Expect similar closures at NDP and NYE; the Floating Platform path was closed for NDP in 2021. — [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/news/cycling-around-marina-bay-is-banned-on-new-years-eve-check-the-specific-locations-here-123024); [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)

**Dismount / cycling rules**
- Dismount at the Clifford Pier driveway and on the Tanjong Rhu suspension bridge (2021 report). — [Little Day Out 2021](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/)
- Henderson Waves and Forest Walk: a third-party guide says pedestrian-only. No NParks statement was found. — [Singapore Spirit](https://www.singapore-spirit.com/guides/southern-ridges-henderson-waves/)
- NParks practice is to dismount and push in No Cycling Zones and crowded areas; see the PCN etiquette FAQ. — [NParks JLG cycling](https://lightsbythelake.nparks.gov.sg/cycling-in-jlg); [PCN etiquette FAQ PDF](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/-/media/nparks-real-content/gardens-parks-and-nature/park-connector-network/00-pcn-overview-page/faqs-on-pcn-etiquette-and-usage.pdf); [Using routes safely](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)

**Hours / etiquette**
- Rail Corridor: open 24/7. 1932 Story Café: Monday 9am–2:30pm, closed Tuesday, Wednesday–Sunday and public holidays 9am–7:30pm. Gallery hours are not stated. — [NParks](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/bukit-timah-railway-station/)
- Sultan Mosque (official): visits Saturday–Thursday 10am–12pm and 2pm–4pm, closed Friday. A third-party site says Friday 2:30–4pm, which conflicts with the official page. Robes are issued first-come-first-served. Remove shoes. Non-Muslims may not enter the main prayer hall. — [sultanmosque.sg](https://sultanmosque.sg/visitors-info); [Forevervacation](https://forevervacation.com/singapore/masjid-sultan); [Singapore Spirit](https://www.singapore-spirit.com/guides/sultan-mosque-guide/)
- Kranji War Memorial: free entry. Hours conflict between sources: 8am–6:30pm (Pacific Wrecks citing CWGC), 7am–6:30pm (TheSmartLocal), 7am–6pm (Time Out). — [Pacific Wrecks](https://img.pacificwrecks.com/cemetery/singapore-kranji.html); [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/kranji-war-memorial/); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/kranji-war-memorial); [CWGC](https://www.cwgc.org/find-a-cemetery/cemetery/4007211/KRANJI%20MILITARY%20CEMETERY)
- Kampong Lorong Buangkok: private, lived-in. A guide reports residents put up a "No Trespassing" sign after pandemic-era intrusions. Go with a guide, take no photos into homes, keep noise low. — [Seek Sophie](https://seeksophie.com/magazine/a/a-trip-to-singapores-last-kampong--lorong-buangkok)

### Inferences
- The app should show "Push bike" badges on Henderson Waves, Forest Walk, the Tanjong Rhu suspension bridge and the Clifford Pier driveway. It should also show a dated closure banner for the Rail Corridor south (Henderson–Spooner) segment.
- For the heritage persona, the app should show the dress code and Friday closure at Sultan Mosque, the conflicting Kranji hours (advise arriving before 6pm), and a "living community, be respectful" note at Lorong Buangkok.

### Gaps
- No official NParks statement on cycling across Henderson Waves or Alexandra Arch was found. The NParks Southern Ridges URL tried returned 404.
- No end date for the Rail Corridor south closure.
- No 2026 confirmation of Reflections at Bukit Chandu hours or of the Bukit Timah Railway Station gallery hours.
- Marina Bay dismount rules come from a 2021 blog and should be re-checked against current signage.
