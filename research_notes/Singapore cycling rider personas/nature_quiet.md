# Singapore leisure cycling personas: "Nature lover / photographer" and "Quiet ride / fitness"

Research date: 20 Nov 2026. Scope: the NParks Park Connector Network (PCN), Round Island Route (RIR) and Coast-to-Coast (C2C) trail. Sources are tagged with their publish or update date where one is known. **None of the sources gave a lat/lon, so this file has no coordinates.** Locations are given as written descriptions only. Geocode them from OneMap or NParks maps before using them in the app.

Note on method: some findings come from search-engine summaries of pages, not from full fetches. These are marked "(search summary)". Treat them as lower confidence than the fetched pages: NParks PCN notices, the NParks "Using Our Routes and Trails Safely" page, the Rail Corridor FAQ, the TheSmartLocal sunrise/sunset list and the LittleDayOut Punggol bridges article.

## Which spots do Singapore photographers and cyclists most recommend for scenic/photo stops on connectors?

### Takeaway
The spots named again and again are in the north-east (Lorong Halus red bridge, the Punggol Waterway bridges, Rower's Bay and Lower Seletar), the north (Ulu Sembawang, Mandai, Sembawang, Woodlands Waterfront), the bay (Marina Barrage, Tanjong Rhu) and the south (Mount Faber/Southern Ridges, Labrador, Keppel Bay). Most sit on or right beside a named park connector or the RIR.

### What the "nature lover / photographer" wants
These riders want a slow ride with many stops. They are after water reflections and golden-hour light, bridges or structures that frame the shot, and wildlife (otters, hornbills, herons, monitor lizards). They need somewhere safe to park the bike close to the viewpoint. They don't mind detours and will start before dawn or stay out until dusk. This profile is inferred from the reasons the sources below give for recommending each spot.

### Cited Findings

Candidate spots for the photographer persona (all locations are descriptive, with no coordinates):

1. **Lorong Halus Wetland red bridge** (sunrise and sunset). The red bridge is the main photo spot. You can walk or cycle there from Punggol Waterway Park, and around 40 bird species have been recorded. Access is LRT from Punggol to Kadaloor, then about 18 min on foot. Nearest PCN: Punggol Waterway / North Eastern Riverine Loop. — [TheSmartLocal, upd. 17 May 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). The red steel structure frames the sunrise, and lalang fields sit beside it. — [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/where-to-watch-the-sunrise-in-singapore). Light reflecting off the water at sunrise and sunset is its selling point. — [Pixeo](https://pixeoapp.com/location/lorong-halus-bridge/8e0de17c9600baeb9ac94e3c0344ab3512391952) (search summary)
2. **Punggol Waterway: Sunrise Bridge** (sunrise). It marks the eastern end of the waterway, where it meets Sungei Serangoon, and you can see the Lorong Halus bridge from it. — [LittleDayOut, 30 Oct 2012 (old)](https://www.littledayout.com/punggol-waterway-review-a-tale-of-five-bridges/). TheSmartLocal names the Sunrise Bridge for sunrise and the Sunset Strip with Jewel Bridge for sunset. It describes the park as an 8 km promenade about 5 min from Punggol MRT. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
3. **Punggol Waterway: Jewel Bridge / Sunset Strip** (sunset). The bridge has a birdcage-like top and sits at the western end, where the waterway meets Sungei Punggol. The harp-like road bridge on the Sunset Strip is another landmark. — [LittleDayOut, 2012 (old)](https://www.littledayout.com/punggol-waterway-review-a-tale-of-five-bridges/)
4. **Rower's Bay Park** (sunset). It has a wetland boardwalk and is "along the Round Island Route". Bring insect repellent. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). It is also an RIR node and the C2C launch site. — [MND speech](https://mnd.gov.sg/newsroom/speeches/view/speech-by-minister-lawrence-wong-at-the-launch-of-the-round-island-route-node-at-rower-s-bay-and-coast-to-coast-trail) (search summary)
5. **Lower Seletar Reservoir Park: Heritage Bridge and the viewing deck at the end of the platform** (sunset). Photographers favour it, and it is about 13 min on foot from Khatib MRT. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). Nearest PCN: Northern Explorer Loop.
6. **Yishun Dam** (sunset). It has seating on the breakwater, and planes from Seletar Airport fly past. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). It sits on the RIR, west of Rower's Bay (inferred from the shared bus access in the same article).
7. **Ulu Sembawang Park Connector** (sunrise mist). Morning mist is the attraction. It links the Woodlands (SLE) PC to the Mandai PC. It is open 24/7 but has **no street lights**, so go in daylight. — [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/ulu-sembawang-park-connector/) (search summary). NParks gives it as about 1.3 km. — [NParks Ulu Sembawang PC](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/ulu-sembawang-pc/) (search summary). Sunrise-ride write-up: [SengkangBabies](https://sengkangbabies.com/tag/how-to-go-to-ulu-sembawang)
8. **Mandai Park Connector** (forest edge). It is about 5 km long and runs beside the Central Catchment Nature Reserve, linking Ulu Sembawang PC to Khatib Bongsu PC. It is lit from 7pm to 7am. — [NParks Mandai PC](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/mandai-pc/) (search summary)
9. **Kranji Reservoir Park** (sunrise and sunset). It offers wildlife, picnic space and Battle of Kranji memorial plaques. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
10. **Woodlands Waterfront Park** (sunrise and sunset). It looks out over the Straits of Johor and is about 10 min from Woodlands North station. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). An LTA release of 14 Nov 2026 says new Woodlands cycling paths connect residents to the PCN and RIR Phase 1. — [LTA, Nov 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/11/news-releases/16km-of-cycling-paths-in-woodlands-completed.html) (search summary)
11. **Sembawang Park** (sunrise and sunset). A quiet beachside park with old war bunkers. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
12. **Changi Bay Point** (sunrise). It has sea-facing benches and a lookout point on the connector stretch between Changi Beach and East Coast Park (Changi Airport Connector / RIR). — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/where-to-watch-the-sunrise-in-singapore)
13. **Changi Boardwalk** (Sunset Walk faces west, and sunrise works anywhere along it). — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). Cycling is banned on some Changi Beach Park boardwalks, so riders should park and walk. — [NParks sharing PCN, upd. 6 Oct 2026](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
14. **East Coast Park: Bedok Jetty and the breakwater near carpark F2** (sunrise; you can also see sunset because the sun sets on your right). It gets crowded on weekends. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
15. **Marina Barrage** (sunrise and sunset). It has a rooftop lawn and the best skyline panorama at sunset. Bay East Garden is quieter. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). Marina Bay is reachable 24h via the Marina Promenade PC. — (search summary of [TheSmartLocal PCN list](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/park-connectors-singapore/))
16. **Tanjong Rhu Promenade / suspension bridge** (sunset). Less crowded, with reflections of the city and a lookout tower nearby. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). The Tanjong Rhu PC has a suspension bridge. — (search summary)
17. **Kallang River / Kallang Riverside (Sports Hub waterfront)** (otters). Kallang PC is about 7.87 km and links Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park to Kallang Riverside Park, with birds, monitor lizards and squirrels along the bank. — [NParks Kallang PC](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/kallang-pc/) (search summary). Best otter-spotting times are 7–9am and 4–6pm. — [The Kallang](https://thekallang.com.sg/explore/spot-otters-and-other-local-wildlife-along-singapore-sports-hubs-waterfront) (search summary)
18. **Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park (Kallang River naturalised channel)** (otters). It is home to a well-known smooth-coated otter family. — (search summary of [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/wild-animals-singapore-crocodiles-dolphins-otters/)). It sits on the Kallang PC / C2C.
19. **Pasir Ris Park** (hornbills, otters, mangrove). It is one of the most reliable places to find Oriental Pied Hornbills, and nesting has been observed. — [10,000 Birds](https://www.10000birds.com/pasir-ris-park-singapore.htm) (search summary). Otters have been seen from beside the Pasir Ris PC. — (search summary). Cycling is **banned on the mangrove boardwalks**. — [NParks, upd. Oct 2026](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/). It is on the RIR and the 18 km Eastern Corridor. — [NParks Eastern Corridor](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/news/news-detail/completion-of-the-eastern-corridor)
20. **Mount Faber / Faber Point** (sunrise and sunset). It has city and harbour views, and a trail runs about 10 km to Labrador Park. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). Southern Ridges Loop.
21. **Henderson Waves**. It is a pedestrian bridge over Henderson Road linking the Southern Ridges and Mount Faber, and its infobox lists cyclists as users. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henderson_Waves). NParks bans cycling on the "Henderson Wave Bridge Forest Walk (Telok Blangah Hill Park)". — [NParks, Oct 2026](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/). It is unclear whether that ban covers the Waves deck itself. See Gaps.
22. **Keppel Bay** (sunrise and sunset, with a marina backdrop) and **Labrador Park** (rocky shore). — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). The Labrador nature reserve and some Berlayer Creek boardwalks are no-cycling. — [NParks, Oct 2026](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
23. **Jurong Lake Gardens** (C2C start). The C2C trail starts here and runs 36 km to Coney Island Park. — (search summary of NParks JLG pages). JLG has an official no-cycling-zone map covering the Lakeside Green boardwalk, Forest Ramble and Grasslands. — [JLG No Cycling Zones PDF](https://juronglakegardens.nparks.gov.sg/files/Maps%20and%20Trails/JLG_No_Cycling_Zones.pdf) (search summary). Expect large crowds during Lights by the Lake 2026. — (search summary of [lightsbythelake.nparks.gov.sg](https://lightsbythelake.nparks.gov.sg/advisories/)). The cycling page returned 404 when fetched.
24. **Lower Peirce Reservoir** (sunset). The pavilion is 3 min from the carpark. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). This one is probably not a cycling stop: it sits by the Central Catchment, where cycling is banned. See Inferences.
25. **Raffles Marina Lighthouse** (sunset, Tuas). — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/). It is in the far west, and no PCN link was confirmed.

Other context:
- TheSmartLocal gives golden hour as roughly 6:45–7:15am for sunrise and 6–7pm for sunset. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
- The Tuas lalang field, once a popular shoot location, is listed as **closed**. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)

### Inferences
- The north-east (Punggol, Lorong Halus, Rower's Bay, Lower Seletar, Yishun Dam) packs the most sunrise and sunset spots into one stretch of RIR. It is the best "photo ride" cluster for this persona.
- Lower Peirce sits beside the Central Catchment, where cycling is banned. It makes more sense as a walking side trip than as a ride stop.
- Riders will need to dismount at several of the best viewpoints (Punggol bridges, Pasir Ris mangrove boardwalk, Changi boardwalks, JLG zones). The app should show "park bike, walk X m" at these spots.

### Gaps
- No sourced lat/lon for any spot. Geocode them from OneMap or NParks.
- It is unclear whether Henderson Waves itself is no-cycling or only the adjacent "Forest Walk". The NParks wording is ambiguous, and I found no Southern Ridges cycling page.
- The Instagram, Reddit r/singapore and Straits Times photo guides could not be fetched or searched in depth within budget.
- I found no source rating heron spots specifically. They are a common sight at Kranji, Sungei Buloh and Punggol, but I could not cite that.

## Which are best at sunrise/sunset or for wildlife?

### Takeaway
East- and north-east-facing water spots work best at sunrise: Changi Bay Point, Changi Boardwalk, East Coast Park, Lorong Halus and Punggol's Sunrise Bridge. Spots facing west or the Johor Strait work best at sunset: Rower's Bay, Lower Seletar, Yishun Dam, Jewel Bridge, Tanjong Rhu and Marina Barrage. For wildlife, Pasir Ris is best for hornbills plus otters, and the Kallang River and Bishan stretches are best for otters.

### Cited Findings
- **Sunrise only:** Changi Bay Point, East Coast Park and Pulau Ubin (take the 5:30am ferry). — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
- **Sunset only:** Lower Peirce, Raffles Marina, Rower's Bay, Lower Seletar, Tanjong Rhu and Yishun Dam. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
- **Both sunrise and sunset:** Lorong Halus, Kranji Reservoir, Mount Faber, Woodlands Waterfront, Keppel Bay, Sembawang Park, Marina Barrage, Punggol Waterway, Merlion Park, Changi Boardwalk, Labrador and Sentosa. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
- **Otters on the Kallang River:** best 7–9am and 4–6pm. — [The Kallang](https://thekallang.com.sg/explore/spot-otters-and-other-local-wildlife-along-singapore-sports-hubs-waterfront) (search summary)
- **Hornbills:** reported at Pasir Ris, Pulau Ubin, Central Catchment, Sungei Buloh and St John's. No reports along the Kallang or Bishan stretch. — (search summary of [10,000 Birds](https://www.10000birds.com/pasir-ris-park-singapore.htm) and other results)
- **Lorong Halus:** around 40 bird species. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
- **NParks safety advisories (current Nov 2026):** wild boars at Punggol Promenade, managed intertidal access at Punggol Point, and box jellyfish sightings. — [NParks PCN notices, upd. 16 Nov 2026](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/notices)

### Inferences
- A good "sunrise to sunset" pattern for photographers is to start at Lorong Halus or Punggol at dawn and finish at Rower's Bay, Lower Seletar or Yishun Dam at dusk, all on the north-east RIR.
- Otter timing (early morning and late afternoon) overlaps with golden hour, so a single ride window serves both interests.

### Gaps
- I did not find iNaturalist or NParks Biome sighting data that could give spot-level wildlife frequency.
- No sourced sunrise or sunset azimuths per spot.

## Which connector stretches are quietest and best for uninterrupted riding?

### Takeaway
The northern forest-edge connectors (Mandai PC, Ulu Sembawang PC, Lower Seletar / Northern Explorer Loop) are the quietest and hilliest. For long rides the options are the 75 km RIR eastern half and the 18 km Eastern Corridor. The Southern Ridges and Mandai Road give the main climbs. East Coast Park, Marina Bay and Punggol are busy on weekends.

### What the "quiet ride / fitness" rider wants
These riders want long stretches with no crossings or dismount points, low pedestrian density, some rolling gradient, lighting if they ride early or late, and predictable times when the paths are quiet. This profile is inferred from the reasons the sources give.

### Cited Findings
- **Mandai PC:** about 5 km along the Central Catchment edge, lit 7pm–7am. **Mandai Road** is "famous for leg and lung workout". — [NParks Mandai PC](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/mandai-pc/) (search summary); [SengkangBabies](https://sengkangbabies.com/tag/cycling-at-tuas-map) (search summary)
- **Ulu Sembawang PC:** slopes, and no lighting after sunset. — (search summary of [SengkangBabies](https://sengkangbabies.com/tag/how-to-go-to-ulu-sembawang) and [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/ulu-sembawang-park-connector/))
- **Northern Explorer Loop:** starts at Lower Seletar Reservoir Park and "will lead you away from the busy Yishun heartlands". Sources disagree on its length (11 km vs 25 km). — (search summary of [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-in-singapore/) and [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/the-best-cycling-trails-in-singapore))
- **Kranji to Lim Chu Kang countryside (about 20 km):** farms, Kranji War Memorial and Sungei Buloh. There are **no pavements and many trucks on weekdays**, so weekends are safer. — (search summary of [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-in-singapore/) / [SethLui](https://sethlui.com/cycling-routes-singapore-guide/))
- **RIR Phase 1:** 75 km across the eastern half, Seletar to Labrador, opened 22 Jan 2022. The full roughly 150 km loop is due by 2035. — [NParks, Jan 2022](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2022/1/nparks-completes-phase-one-of-the-round-island-route-with-75km-of-recreational-connection-stretching-across-eastern-half-of-singapore); [Time Out](https://timeout.com/singapore/news/phase-1-of-the-round-island-route-that-stretches-75km-from-seletar-to-labrador-is-now-open-012422). I found no 2026 announcement of new RIR kilometres.
- **Eastern Corridor:** 18 km linking Pasir Ris Park and East Coast Park via Bedok Reservoir. NParks planned three new cycling bridges at Changi Beach Park and East Coast Park (about Feb 2024). — [NParks](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/news/news-detail/completion-of-the-eastern-corridor); [Alvinology, Feb 2024](https://alvinology.com/2024/02/24/eastern-corridor-completed-with-enhanced-recreational-experience-round-island-route-set-for-upgrades/amp)
- **Rail Corridor:** a long, continuous north–south route (one source cites about 24 km from Kranji to the Bukit Timah truss bridge via Mandai PC). See the closures section for its 2026 status. — (search summary)
- **Busy times and places:** East Coast Park is crowded on weekends, and Merlion Park / Marina Bay "can be crowded". Arrive early at Marina Barrage, and use Bay East Garden for a quieter option. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
- **NParks etiquette:** no numeric speed limit is given. Riders should slow down and give way in crowded or narrow areas, ride single file, use lights at night, and not overtake near crossings or bends. — [NParks sharing PCN, upd. 6 Oct 2026](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)

### Inferences
- **Best quiet and uninterrupted riding:** Mandai PC plus Ulu Sembawang plus Lower Seletar / Northern Explorer on weekday mornings. Mandai Road and the Southern Ridges approach (Mount Faber) offer the only real climbing.
- **Best long continuous ride:** RIR Phase 1 (Seletar to Punggol to Pasir Ris to Changi to East Coast to Marina). It is busiest around East Coast Park and Marina on weekends, so fitness riders should do those sections before about 7:30am or on weekdays.
- **Places to avoid, given their dismount points:** Punggol Waterway bridges, Pasir Ris boardwalks, the interior of Jurong Lake Gardens, and Marina Bay on weekend evenings.

### Gaps
- No Strava heatmap or counts data was found, so "quietest" rests on blog descriptions only.
- No sourced gradients or elevation figures for the Southern Ridges or Mandai.
- The Northern Explorer Loop length conflicts between sources (11 km vs 25 km).

## Any closures or no-cycling restrictions as of 2026?

### Takeaway
Several multi-year PCN closures are active as of Nov 2026 (Ang Mo Kio, Kallang, Loyang, Bedok, Alexandra-Queensway, and C2C at Ang Mo Kio). The Rail Corridor's Henderson Road–Spooner Road stretch is closed until July 2027, and cycling is banned there. Cycling is also banned in nature reserves, on many boardwalks, on select Punggol bridges and in most of Jurong Lake Gardens.

**Rail Corridor** ([Rail Corridor FAQ](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/faq/)):
- The Jalan Kilang Barat–Henderson Road section reopened on 2 Mar 2026, ahead of its 31 Jul 2026 target.
- The Henderson Road–Spooner Road section is closed, with reopening expected **31 Jul 2027**. "Walking, jogging and cycling are not allowed within the closed section."
- The FAQ is internally inconsistent on the start date (9 Mar vs 2 Mar 2026).

**No-cycling areas** ([NParks sharing PCN, upd. 6 Oct 2026](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)):
- Nature reserves: Bukit Timah, Central Catchment, Sungei Buloh, Labrador.
- Parks and walks: Henderson Wave Bridge Forest Walk (Telok Blangah Hill), Canopy Walk (Kent Ridge), Hindhede, Singapore Botanic Gardens, Thomson Nature Park, Rifle Range Nature Park, and the Chestnut Nature Park hiking trails.
- Jurong Lake Gardens: no cycling except one east–west path. This item may sit under a list that applies to PMDs and PABs. Bicycle-specific zones are in the [JLG No Cycling Zones PDF](https://juronglakegardens.nparks.gov.sg/files/Maps%20and%20Trails/JLG_No_Cycling_Zones.pdf).
- Boardwalks: Pasir Ris mangrove, West Coast Park Marsh Garden, **Coney Island**, Admiralty Park, select Bukit Chermin / Berlayer Creek boardwalks, Changi Beach Park and Windsor Nature Park.
- Punggol Waterway Park: **Kelong, Wave and Adventure bridges** and a boardwalk.
- Dairy Farm Nature Park: Wallace Trail.

**Other:**
- The Tuas lalang field (a former photo spot) is closed. — [TheSmartLocal, 2024](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/sunrise-sunset-spots-singapore/)
- A 2025 Ulu Pandan PC closure ran 17–29 Nov 2025 and is now expired. — [NParks PDF](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/UPPC_section_closure_17_29_Nov_2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Coney Island boardwalk ban matters because Coney Island is the C2C's eastern end. C2C riders reach the end point but should expect to push or park inside the island.
- The Bedok closures are due to end on 31 Dec 2026. The app should re-check them in Jan 2027.
- Ang Mo Kio C2C disruptions last until 2028, which affects the C2C Central segment through Bishan and AMK, including the otter spots.

### Gaps
- It is ambiguous whether the Jurong Lake Gardens ban covers bicycles as well as PMDs and PABs. The Lights by the Lake cycling page returned 404.
- It is ambiguous whether Henderson Waves itself is in the no-cycling zone.
- No Straits Times 2026 coverage of new restrictions was checked within budget.
