# Tourist and Family-with-Kids Rider Personas: Singapore Leisure Cycling (PCN, Round Island Route, City Waterfront)

Research date: 20 Nov 2026. No coordinates are given anywhere in these notes. Older sources carry their dates. Bike-share apps, MRT stations and street addresses are named only where a source names them.

## Q1. What would a first-time visitor most want to see by bike, and what's realistic in 2–3 hours?

### Takeaway
The best first ride is a 2–3 hour evening Marina Bay loop of roughly 11 km. It passes Marina Bay Sands, Helix Bridge, the Merlion, the Esplanade, the Singapore Flyer and the Gardens by the Bay Supertrees. The second-best option is a flat out-and-back along the East Coast Park seafront, extended along the Changi Airport Connector to Jewel if time allows. Pulau Ubin needs half a day or more because of the bumboat crossing. The Southern Ridges have dismount-only sections and are not a good bike outing.

### Cited Findings
**Iconic spots reachable by bike (with why):**
1. **Marina Bay Sands / Marina Bay waterfront.** The anchor of the Marina Bay Loop, an easy loop of about 11–11.5 km that passes MBS and the Helix Bridge — [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/cycling-routes-in-singapore/) (via search summary); [Honeycombers, 26 Aug 2024](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/)
2. **Helix Bridge.** On the Marina Bay Loop — [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/cycling-routes-in-singapore/)
3. **Merlion Park.** Photo stop on the Marina Bay loop — [Merewards](https://merewards.sg/stories-details/472/best-cycling-routes-bike-trails-in-singapore)
4. **Gardens by the Bay Supertrees, Esplanade, Singapore Flyer.** All listed as Marina Bay Loop highlights. Honeycombers says to start in the late evening to catch the sunset over the skyline — [Honeycombers, 26 Aug 2024](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/)
5. **Marina Promenade Park Connector / Marina Bay front.** Reachable from Marina Bay MRT and open 24 hours — [Her World](https://www.herworld.com/life/travel/best-leisure-cycling-routes-singapore) (via search summary)
6. **Singapore River / Robertson Quay.** Alexandra Canal PC is flat and canal-side and ends near cafés at Robertson Quay. Some early sections run beside a main road — [Sassy Mama, posted 25 Sep 2026 / footer last-updated Nov 2025](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore). Honeycombers does not cover the Singapore River as a separate route.
7. **East Coast Park seafront.** Flat and beginner-friendly, with rental shops in the park. The Eastern Coastal Loop is described as about 20 km to Changi and 27 km to Marina Bay (VisitSingapore, via search summary; the page itself would not render for fetch) — [VisitSingapore](https://VisitSingapore.com/editorials/cycling-routes-in-singapore/). Honeycombers adds jetty views, Bedok Food Centre and East Coast Lagoon Food Village as highlights and calls it a sunset spot — [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/)
8. **Changi Airport Connector and Changi Jurassic Mile (to Jewel).** A 3.5 km cycling and jogging path from East Coast Park to Changi Airport, starting at HUB & SPOKE near T2 and linking to Jewel. The Jurassic Mile is about 1 km long with more than 20 life-sized prehistoric creatures — [Yahoo/ST, Oct 2020](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/new-cycling-and-jogging-path-links-east-coast-park-to-changi-airport-084810540.html); [Little Day Out](https://www.littledayout.com/jurassic-mile-changi-airport-connector-dinosaur-walk-cafe-bike-rentals/). One guide says you must dismount and push your bike along the Jurassic Mile — [Railtravelstation](https://railtravelstation.com/cycling-to-changi-airport-jurassic-mile/) (blog, unverified)
9. **Free layover bike ride at Changi.** Transit passengers with a layover of at least 5.5 hours and under 24 hours get a free 2-hour GoCycling ride if the layover overlaps operating hours — [Changi Airport](https://www.changiairport.com/en/airport-guide/hub-and-spoke/free-bicycle-rides.html) (via search summary; check the terms are still current in 2026)
10. **Pulau Ubin via the Changi Point Ferry Terminal bumboat.** The island is reached only by bumboat from Changi Point Ferry Terminal, a crossing of about 15 minutes — [Railtravelstation](https://railtravelstation.com/pulau-ubin-bumboat-changi-point-ferry-terminal-to-pulau-ubin-main-jetty-by-bumboat/). The fare is commonly listed as S$4 one way, but a September 2025 report describes a rise to S$5 — [Mothership, Sep 2025](https://mothership.sg/2025/09/man-who-refused-to-pay-increased-s5-pulau-ubin-boat-fare-stranded-for-an-hour-charters-another-boat-for-s48/). NParks says to confirm the fare with the operator — [NParks Pulau Ubin directions](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/pulau-ubin/visit/directions-and-map). Boats run roughly 6am–7pm and leave once about 12 passengers gather. Payment is cash only and there are no ATMs on the island — [Homejourney](https://www.homejourney.sg/blog/pulau-ubin-day-trip-itinerary-guide-homejourney) (commercial blog). Bike shops on the island rent bikes, and you can bring your own on the boat for a surcharge. Ketam Trail is for advanced mountain bikers only — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore)
11. **Round Island Route (eastern half).** About 75 km from Rower's Bay (Seletar) to Berlayer Creek (Labrador). Highlights include Seletar Aerospace Park's black-and-white houses, the Sengkang and Punggol bridges, and Jewel Bridge at Punggol Waterway Park (best at sunrise or sunset) — [Honeycombers, Aug 2024](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/); opened 22 Jan 2022 — [NParks](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2022/1/nparks-completes-phase-one-of-the-round-island-route-with-75km-of-recreational-connection-stretching-across-eastern-half-of-singapore). The full 150 km loop is targeted progressively by 2035. An LTA release of 14 Nov 2026 still refers only to "the first phase" — [LTA, Nov 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/11/news-releases/16km-of-cycling-paths-in-woodlands-completed.html)
12. **Punggol Waterway Park.** Recommended for families and for sunrise or sunset photos — [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-in-singapore/) (via search summary). The Kelong, Wave and Adventure bridges and the boardwalk there are no-cycling zones — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
13. **Southern Ridges (Mount Faber / Telok Blangah / HortPark).** A challenging hill climb with panoramic city views from Mount Faber — [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/). **However, Henderson Waves Bridge and the Forest Walk at Telok Blangah Hill Park are officially no-cycling, as is the Kent Ridge Park Canopy Walk** — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/). Littlesteps says the trail is foot-only — [Littlesteps](https://www.littlestepsasia.com/singapore/articles/play/henderson-waves). Time Out describes a 9 km bike trail through Mount Faber Park and Telok Blangah Hill Park — [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/the-best-cycling-trails-in-singapore) (via search summary). Sources conflict on how rideable the area is.
14. **Sentosa coastline.** Easier coastal routes, one starting at Sentosa Boardwalk beside VivoCity — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore)
15. **Kallang Wave Mall water play, with a skyline view** — [HoneyKids](https://honeykidsasia.com/where-to-go-cycling-in-singapore-best-places-for-a-family-bike-ride/) (via search summary)

**Not allowed for tourists to cycle (relevant to sightseeing):** Singapore Botanic Gardens (a UNESCO site), the four Nature Reserves (Bukit Timah, Central Catchment, Sungei Buloh, Labrador), and most of Jurong Lake Gardens apart from one east-west connecting path — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)

### Inferences
- **Realistic in 2–3 hours:** (a) the Marina Bay Loop of about 11 km at leisure pace with photo stops, ideally starting around late afternoon for sunset; or (b) East Coast Park, riding one way from a rental kiosk toward the Lagoon/Jurassic Mile and back. The full Eastern Coastal Loop to Changi (about 20 km one way) is too long for a 2–3 hour leisure ride that includes stops.
- Pulau Ubin and the full Round Island Route eastern half are day trips, not 2–3 hour rides.
- The app should not route tourists over Henderson Waves or into the Botanic Gardens. It should show these as walk or dismount segments.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the VisitSingapore (STB) cycling page, so its official distances come from a search snippet only.
- I found no official measured length for a "Marina Bay Loop". The 11–11.5 km figure comes from TheSmartLocal.
- I found no 2026 source on Gardens by the Bay perimeter cycling rules (whether riding is allowed inside the Gardens versus on the waterfront promenade).
- TripAdvisor forum sentiment was not reviewed.

## Q2. Which rentals exist in 2026 and where?

### Takeaway
Two dockless bike-share operators are licensed in 2026: Anywheel and HelloRide. Both are app-based and spread island-wide. For park leisure rides, staffed or self-service kiosks run by GoCycling and Coastline Leisure / Bike Stop cluster at East Coast Park, Marina Bay (Singapore Flyer), Changi Airport T2 and Changi Village, and Pasir Ris. Their prices start at about S$10/hr for an adult bike, but those prices are mostly 2024 data.

### Cited Findings
- **Licensed bike-share operators:** A competition-regulator (CCS) release from August 2026 calls Anywheel and HelloRide "the two currently licensed operators of shared bicycle services in Singapore". CCS warned HelloBikeSG for proposing price discussions with Anywheel in 2025 — [CCS](https://www.ccs.gov.sg/media-and-events/newsroom/announcements-and-media-releases/ccs-warns-hellobikesg-pte--ltd--for-attempting-to-propose-price-discussions-with-competitor/)
- **HelloRide licence:** LTA renewed it for up to 20,000 bikes, valid to 30 Jun 2026 — [Stomp/ST, Jul 2025](https://www.stomp.sg/news/bike-sharing-company-helloride-expand-spore-fleet-20000)
- **Fleet sizes:** An August 2026 piece puts the total at 60,000 bikes, 35,000 from Anywheel and 25,000 from HelloRide — [SUTD/Business Times](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/news-listing/no-longer-an-eyesore-but-are-shared-bikes-a-viable-commuting-option-in-singapore/). The 25,000 figure is above the 20,000 cap in the 2025 approval, so I could not reconcile it and I have not confirmed a post-June-2026 renewal.
- **East Coast Park outlets (Little Day Out, 30 Mar 2024; prices "correct as at publication")** — [Little Day Out](https://www.littledayout.com/east-coast-park-bicycle-rental/):
  - Bike Stop @ Fort Road (Area B1), 720 East Coast Pkwy: Sun–Thu 9am–8pm, Fri–Sat 9am–9pm. Rents tandems, children's bikes and adult bikes with child seats, and sells refreshments.
  - Coastline Leisure, 80 Marine Parade Central #01-778 (underpass to Area C): daily 9am–8pm, about S$10–12 for 3 hours.
  - GoCycling @ Carpark C4 kiosk (about 750 m from Cyclist Park): daily 8am–10pm. Adult and child bikes plus quad bikes.
  - Bike Stop @ Lagoon Food Centre (Carpark E2), 1220 East Coast Pkwy: listed as 24 hours. Quad family bikes.
  - GoCycling @ G1, 1490 East Coast Pkwy: self-service, 10am–7pm. You unlock it by sending a message, and a camera monitors the kiosk.
  - GoCycling @ HUB & SPOKE, Changi Airport T2, 60 Airport Blvd #01-T2S-01: Mon–Fri 10am–8pm, Sat/Sun/PH 9am–9pm.
  - GoCycling @ Marina Bay, Singapore Flyer, 30 Raffles Ave #01-01: 9am–9pm. GoCycling bikes can be returned at any GoCycling outlet.
  - Indicative prices: adult from about S$10/hr, child from about S$8/hr, tandem about S$20/hr, quad from about S$40/hr. Some kiosks run "rent one hour, get one free" off-peak.
- **2026 price check:** A guide dated June 2026 lists S$10/hr adult singles at both GoCycling and Coastline / Bike Stop — [The Money Bees](https://themoneybees.co/blog/bicycle-rental-singapore) (unverified blog)
- **Pasir Ris:** A GoCycling Pasir Ris Town Park self-service kiosk from about S$8/hr, and a Momentum Cycle shop near Carpark E from about S$10/hr. One guide notes some earlier outlets have closed — [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bicycle-rental-singapore/) (via search summary). A 2016 NParks notice listed a Coastline Leisure Bike Stop at Pasir Ris Park Carpark C (dated 2016) — [NParks Buzz 2016](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/nparksbuzz/issue-30-vol-3-2016/highlights/upcoming-tenants-activities)
- **Changi Village:** Most rental shops are near Changi Village Hawker Centre. Another guide says GoCycling is the only operator, with two Changi Village outlets plus T2. The sources conflict — [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bicycle-rental-singapore/)
- **Pulau Ubin:** Bike shops on the island, roughly S$8–15/day (blog figure, no official 2026 rate) — [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/destination/things-to-do-in-pulau-ubin-acc/258625)
- **West Coast Park:** Rentals include bikes, go-karts and four-person bikes — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore)

### Inferences
- For tourists starting near MRT stations or hotels, the bike-share apps (Anywheel, HelloRide) are the practical choice in the city core. For East Coast, Pasir Ris and Changi, the kiosks offer kids' bikes, child seats, tandems and quad bikes, which bike-share does not.
- GoCycling's one-way returns (Marina Bay, East Coast, Changi) make a one-way Marina Bay to Changi ride practical. Confirm this is still offered in 2026.

### Gaps
- I did not reach the Anywheel or HelloRide sites for 2026 per-minute pricing, parking-zone rules or whether tourists can register with a foreign phone or card.
- I found no official 2026 confirmation of each kiosk's hours. Most are from March 2024.
- I found no source on helmet rental availability at kiosks.

## Q3. What rules/fines apply to cyclists on paths and roads in 2026?

### Takeaway
Speed limits are 10 km/h on footpaths and 25 km/h on shared and cycling paths. Helmets are mandatory on roads. Cycling is banned on expressways and pedestrian-only paths (in force since 1 Jul 2025, up to S$2,000 and/or 3 months' jail). Riders must dismount at "No Riding" signs, and road groups are capped at 5 single file or 10 two abreast. Folded foldable bikes no larger than 120×70×40 cm may go on MRT and buses. The 1 June 2026 changes target e-scooters and mobility aids, not regular bicycles.

### Cited Findings
- **Speed limits:** footpaths 10 km/h and shared paths 25 km/h (MOT written reply, 24 Feb 2026). LTA and SPF run joint operations and use speed-detection radars and CCTV at hotspots. More than 650 cyclists were penalised from 2023 to 2025 for disorderly riding — [MOT, Feb 2026](https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/measures-to-prevent-speeding-and-reckless-riding-by-cyclists/)
- **Helmets:** Cyclists and PAB riders must wear a helmet when cycling on roads — [LTA Rules & Code of Conduct](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltaweb/en/walk-cycle-ride/rules-and-code-of-conduct.html). On park connectors helmets are advised, not required: "Wear protective gear such as a helmet" — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
- **Pedestrian-only paths (from 1 Jul 2025):** Bicycles and non-motorised PMDs are banned on footpaths that were converted to pedestrian-only and run beside dedicated cycling paths. Standalone footpaths and shared paths (marked with dotted red edge lines) are unaffected — [LTA](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltaweb/en/walk-cycle-ride/rules-and-code-of-conduct.html). First-time offenders face up to S$2,000, 3 months' jail, or both — [Stomp/ST](https://www.stomp.sg/news/cyclists-now-face-fines-jail-riding-pedestrian-only-paths); [blog summary](https://lakopinews.blogspot.com/2025/07/2000-fine-or-jail-for-cycling-and-rider.html)
- **Expressways:** Cycling is banned on expressways, road tunnels and selected viaducts (list in an LTA PDF) — [LTA](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltaweb/en/walk-cycle-ride/rules-and-code-of-conduct.html)
- **Dismount zones:** Dismount and push at "No Riding" signs. Use bicycle lanes and crossings where available, and stop and look before crossing — [LTA](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltaweb/en/walk-cycle-ride/rules-and-code-of-conduct.html). In parks, slow down in Attention Zones (red dotted lines), give way, ride single file, keep both hands on the bars, use lights at night, and do not overtake near crossings or bends — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
- **Group riding on roads:** At most 5 single file or 10 two abreast. Two abreast is not allowed on single-lane roads or during bus-lane hours, and groups should keep a 30 m (2 lamp-post) gap. The cap is exempt on the Tanah Merah Coast Rd and West Camp Rd bicycle lanes on Sundays 5–11am — [LTA](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltaweb/en/walk-cycle-ride/rules-and-code-of-conduct.html)
- **Device rules:** At least one working handbrake. Front white and rear red lights in the dark. Bicycles at most 20 kg and 70 cm wide on paths (130 cm wide and 260 cm long on roads). PAB motor assistance must cut off at 25 km/h, and e-scooters must be UL2272 certified with a 25 km/h maximum. E-scooter riders under 16 must not use cycling paths unless supervised by someone 21 or older. PAB riders under 16 may not ride on cycling paths or roads, and PAB riders may not carry passengers under 16 there — [LTA](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltaweb/en/walk-cycle-ride/rules-and-code-of-conduct.html)
- **From 1 Jun 2026:** Keeping a non-UL2272 e-scooter is an offence (up to S$2,000 and/or 3 months for first offenders). Enhanced personal mobility aid (PMA) rules also apply, including a Certificate of Medical Need. From 1 Jun to 31 Jul 2026 there were 21 non-UL2272 e-scooter cases, 5 PMA speeding cases and 3 riders without a Certificate of Medical Need. Mobility scooter registration starts in mid-2027, and riding an unregistered one becomes an offence from 1 Jan 2029 — [LTA factsheet, Aug 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/8/news-releases/enforcement-operations-enhanced-active-mobility-regulations.html)
- **Enforcement example:** A joint LTA/SPF operation on 14 Aug 2026 near Jalan Anak Bukit and West Coast Highway issued composition fines to 17 cyclists and 1 PMD user — [SPF, Aug 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/Media-Hub/News/2026/08/20260823_joint_enforcement_operation_against_errant_cyclists). MOT reports 60 enforcement cameras island-wide — [MOT](https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/procurement-and-deployment-of-active-mobility-enforcement-cameras--and-data-on-fines-issued-under-enhanced-regulations/)
- **No-cycling park locations:** Nature Reserves, Henderson Waves, the Telok Blangah Forest Walk, the Kent Ridge Canopy Walk, the Botanic Gardens, Jurong Lake Gardens (except one path), the Pasir Ris mangrove boardwalks, the Coney Island boardwalk, the Changi Beach Park boardwalks, the Punggol Waterway Park bridges and boardwalk, and others. The MyTransport.SG app's "Cycling Routes" tab shows the permitted paths — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
- **Bikes on MRT and buses:** Only foldable bikes, folded and no larger than 120×70×40 cm. They must stay folded in stations, trains and buses, you may not ride inside stations, and you must use the wide fare gates — [LTA PDF](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/ltagov/getting_around/active_mobility/pdf/foldable_bike_and_pmds_on_public_transport.pdf). Sources conflict on whether this is all-day or off-peak only. Tower Transit's announcement says "all day" — [Tower Transit](https://www.towertransit.sg/announcements/bring-your-foldable-bicycles-and-personal-mobility-devices-on-board-public-transport-all-day); older mobot.sg says off-peak only — [mobot.sg](https://mobot.sg/?p=2025). Non-foldable bikes (including bike-share bikes) are not covered by the scheme.

### Inferences
- The most important rules to show tourists are: helmet on roads, stay off expressways, keep to 10 km/h on footpaths, walk at "No Riding" and pedestrian-only signs, walk across Henderson Waves, no riding in the Botanic Gardens, and lights at night.
- Bike-share bikes are not foldable, so tourists cannot take them on the MRT. Plan one-way rides around parking and return rules.

### Gaps
- I found no official 2026 schedule of composition fine amounts for common cyclist offences (speeding on a footpath, no helmet on a road, no lights). Only the S$2,000 / 3 months maximum for pedestrian-only paths is sourced.
- The LTA page states no speed limit for "cycling paths" specifically, and MOT states 25 km/h only for "shared paths". I did not confirm a separate cycling-path figure.
- I did not confirm on an LTA page whether foldable bikes are allowed all day in 2026.
- I did not check road-crossing rules at signalised junctions, or the full list of roads closed to cyclists.

## Q4. Which stretches suit young kids? (playgrounds, water play, toilets, food, stretches to avoid, closures 2026)

### Takeaway
Families with young kids do best on short, flat, mostly car-free park loops with a playground or water play at the end. The best picks are Punggol Waterway Park (4.2 km), the East Coast Park Cyclist Park / Raintree Cove area, Bishan-AMK Park, West Coast Park, Pasir Ris Park and Woodlands Waterfront. Avoid the Southern Ridges climbs, Kranji roads, Ketam Trail, the Rail Corridor gravel and Bedok Reservoir's pebbly lap with stabiliser bikes. Check the 2026 closures at Bedok PC, Kallang PC and Alexandra-Queensway PC.

### Cited Findings
- **Punggol Waterway Park:** 4.2 km, easy and leisurely. Has a water playground and sand play, with restaurants around Punggol Container Park — [Sassy Mama, Sep 2026](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore). Its bridges and boardwalk are no-cycling — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/). NParks advisories mention wild boars at Punggol Promenade Nature Walk and box jellyfish at Punggol Point Walk beaches — [NParks PCN notices](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/notices)
- **East Coast Park:** East Coast Cyclist Park is a free 5.4 ha learn-to-cycle area with an obstacle course and pump track, and it is busy on weekends. Riders keep left and share the path with skaters and runners. Food is at East Coast Lagoon Food Village — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore). Playgrounds are at Katong Park (near Fort Road) and Raintree Cove (near C4). Cyclist Park has a nature playgarden and eateries but no rental shop, only repairs. Bike Stop at Fort Road rents kids' bikes and child seats — [Little Day Out, Mar 2024](https://www.littledayout.com/east-coast-park-bicycle-rental/). The playground spreads across eight play zones — [HoneyKids](https://honeykidsasia.com/where-to-go-cycling-in-singapore-best-places-for-a-family-bike-ride/) (via search summary). NParks Cyclist Park page — [NParks](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/east-coast-park/amenities/cyclist-park)
- **Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park:** Flat, smooth paths. Water playground with sluice gates, adventure and inclusive playgrounds, cafés in the park — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore)
- **West Coast Park:** Flat and quiet, especially on weekdays, with a playground and rentals including four-person bikes — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore). The Marsh Garden Boardwalk there is no-cycling — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
- **Ulu Pandan PC:** Flat and suits balance bikes. Passes Dover MRT, with Buona Vista and Jurong East at the ends — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore)
- **Alexandra Canal PC:** Flat and canal-side. Some early sections run beside a main road, and riders share the path with joggers — [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore). It is about 1.7 km with starter paths — [HoneyKids](https://honeykidsasia.com/where-to-go-cycling-in-singapore-best-places-for-a-family-bike-ride/) (via search summary)
- **Pasir Ris Park:** The cycling track (about 7 km) is well maintained and relatively flat — [HoneyKids](https://honeykidsasia.com/where-to-go-cycling-in-singapore-best-places-for-a-family-bike-ride/) (via search summary). The mangrove boardwalks are no-cycling — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
- **Woodlands Waterfront Park:** A 1.5 km promenade with cycling tracks and a playground, and a water playground nearby. An older source said it was closed during the pandemic, so check its status — [HoneyKids](https://honeykidsasia.com/where-to-go-cycling-in-singapore-best-places-for-a-family-bike-ride/) (via search summary). Admiralty Park next door has a slide-heavy playground, but its boardwalk is no-cycling — [NParks PCN](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/). LTA completed 16 km of Woodlands cycling paths in Nov 2026 — [LTA, Nov 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/11/news-releases/16km-of-cycling-paths-in-woodlands-completed.html)
- **Kallang Wave Mall Splash-N-Surf:** A free water playground with a skyline view, good for cooling off after a ride — [HoneyKids](https://honeykidsasia.com/where-to-go-cycling-in-singapore-best-places-for-a-family-bike-ride/) (via search summary)
- **Changi Jurassic Mile:** Dinosaurs are a strong draw for kids. Rentals and food are at HUB & SPOKE, T2 — [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/); bikes may need to be walked through the section — [Railtravelstation](https://railtravelstation.com/cycling-to-changi-airport-jurassic-mile/)
- **Sengkang Riverside Park:** Has a playground and a park connector path — [Littlesteps](https://www.littlestepsasia.com/singapore/play/outdoor-activities/sengkang-riverside-park/)
- **Stretches to avoid with young kids:**
  - Southern Ridges: a challenging hill climb, and Henderson Waves and Forest Walk are no-cycling — [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/); [NParks](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
  - Bedok Reservoir Park: a 4.3 km lap of mixed pavement and pebbly path that is "tough for bikes with stabilisers" (its playground is one of the largest, though)
  - Coney Island: pebbly and gravelly, needs sturdy bikes and mosquito spray
  - Rail Corridor: gravel, closures that need bikes lifted over steps, and road crossings
  - Kranji: no dedicated cycle paths, so only for road-safe older teens
  - Pulau Ubin: some routes steep, and Ketam Trail is for advanced riders
  - East Coast Park on weekend afternoons: crowded

  Except where cited otherwise above, these come from [Sassy Mama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/best-kids-cycling-routes-singapore).

### Inferences
- For a family persona the app should prefer park-interior paths and enclosed loops (Cyclist Park, Punggol Waterway, Bishan-AMK). It should flag segments beside main roads (early Alexandra Canal PC), gravel (Rail Corridor, Coney, Ubin), climbs (Southern Ridges) and no-cycling boardwalks and bridges as walk segments.
- The Bedok PC closures matter for families linking East Coast Park to Bedok Reservoir until the end of 2026.

### Gaps
- None of the sources lists family toilets or nursing rooms per connector. NParks park pages (not fetched) would be the source for those.
- Time Out's kid-friendly cycling page returned 403, so it was not used.
- I found no 2026 operating status or hours for the water playgrounds (Punggol, Bishan-AMK, Woodlands, Jurong Lake Gardens, Splash-N-Surf). Water playgrounds commonly close for maintenance days, so check before going.
- **Heat, rain and time of day:** Sources only suggest early morning or late afternoon and sunset rides — [Honeycombers](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/cycling-routes-singapore-east-coast-pulau-ubin-marina-bay/); general guides via search summary. I found no NEA or STB source on heat-stress thresholds, lightning-risk protocols or monsoon timing for cyclists. The app should take heat and lightning alerts from NEA or Meteorological Service Singapore data rather than these notes.
