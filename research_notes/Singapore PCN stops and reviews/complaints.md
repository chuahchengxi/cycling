# Singapore PCN / Round Island Route: cyclist complaints, problem stops, closures (as of 20 Nov 2026)

Method note: 15 web searches/fetches. Reddit, HardwareZone and Strava threads did not show up in search results (no thread could be retrieved), so cyclist sentiment below comes mostly from news sites (Mothership, MustShareNews, Stomp), blogs, and official NParks/URA/NEA notices. **No coordinates were found in any source, so lat/lon is left as "not sourced" everywhere. Geocode from official addresses rather than guessing.** Where a finding comes from a search-engine summary and not a fetched page, it is marked "(via search summary)".

## Which named spots near PCN routes are widely described as overrated, disappointing, dirty, crowded or closed?

### Takeaway
The most consistent negatives are (1) weekend crowding and cyclist-pedestrian conflict at East Coast Park (around Lagoon Food Village / Marine Cove) and the Rail Corridor, and (2) a long list of official 2026 closures and diversions: Punggol Point Park (to end-2027), Loyang PC (to 2029), two Bedok PC sections, Kallang PC, Ang Mo Kio C2C sections, Alexandra-Queensway PC and the Gardens by the Bay Waterfront Promenade. I found no source calling a specific PCN attraction "overrated". Complaints are about crowds, litter and access, not about how good a place is.

## What facilities and spacing do Singapore cyclists consider essential for rest stops on long rides?

### Takeaway
Riders plan stops every 10–20 km on the Round Island Route, using petrol stations, coffeeshops and park toilet blocks. The essentials are drinking water, a toilet, shade or air-conditioning, and food. Several guides warn that parts of the RIR have few stops.

### Cited Findings
- Bikemap RIR routes plan drinking-water stops at petrol stations (Caltex, Shell, Esso, SPC) and coffeeshops roughly every 10–20 km. Examples: Shell Loyang, the 19 Marsiling Lane coffeeshop, SPC Jalan Buroh — [Bikemap Round the Island](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/8131159); [Bikemap SG RTI](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/9986049) (via search summary)
- A rider guide lists toilets and water coolers at Rower's Bay, Sengkang Riverside Park, Punggol Waterfront, Lorong Halus, Pasir Ris Park and Changi Point Ferry Terminal — [Sengkangbabies RIR map](https://sengkangbabies.com/tag/round-island-cycling-map) (via search summary)
- East Coast Park has "plenty of public toilets, vending machines and food stalls along the entire stretch" — [GetGo blog](https://getgo.sg/blog/best-cycling-routes-singapore) (via search summary; exact source among results uncertain)
- RIR warnings: "there aren't many rest stops along the way so plan ahead", and carry enough water because there often aren't many shops — [TheSmartLocal RIR guide](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/round-island-route/) (via search summary)
- Humidity means riders should expect to drink a couple of litres over a long ride; one guide suggests an early start and at least one air-conditioned stop — [r/SingaporeCycling thread](https://redlib.nohost.network/r/SingaporeCycling/comments/15qux8q/tourist_cycling_rti_sun_aug_3) (via search summary)
- Long-distance cycling guidance (New Zealand, not Singapore) suggests rest areas every 30–40 km with water and toilets, a useful upper bound — [NZTA](https://nzta.govt.nz/walking-cycling-and-public-transport/cycling/cycling-standards-and-guidance/cycling-network-guidance/cycle-network-and-route-planning-guide/principles/complementary-facilities)
- Bike parking is a pain point at popular food stops: it was hard to get a parking lot at Changi Village HC on a public holiday morning — [Sengkangbabies](https://sengkangbabies.com/tag/cycling-to-changi-airport)

### Inferences
- A practical rule for the app: on rides over 20 km, offer a stop every 10–15 km and never let the gap exceed about 20 km. Rank stops in this order: water + toilet + shade, then food, then bike parking. Petrol station kiosks are a valid fallback for water and air-con where there is no park toilet.
- Crowded hawker centres (Changi Village, Lagoon) should be weighted down on weekend and public-holiday mornings.

### Gaps
- No Singapore survey or forum consensus with an exact preferred spacing was found. The 10–20 km figure comes from route planners' choices, not from stated preferences.

## Which official NParks facilities have known recurring problems?

### Takeaway
The documented recurring problems are: toilets only at selected PCN sites, so riders are pointed to malls and food centres; few water coolers on popular routes; shared-path conflict where markings are inconsistent; and litter after long weekends at ECP. I found no NParks-specific reports of broken water coolers or closed toilets.

### Cited Findings
- MND/NParks says toilets are only at selected PCN sites, not along the whole network. It improved signs to nearby mall and food-centre toilets and plans more PCN toilets (2015 written answer) — [MND](https://mnd.gov.sg/newsroom/speeches/view/written-answer-by-ministry-of-national-development-on-toilets-at-park-connectors)
- A 2024 Public Toilets Taskforce is reviewing island-wide toilet cleanliness — [Taskforce report PDF](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/381/bad2d021-4e86-4099-ade4-f683b4f6c865/Public_Toilets_Taskforce_Report.pdf_safe.pdf); [SFA 2025](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/news-publications/newsroom/2025/authorities-remain-committed-to-raising-the-standards-of-public-toilet-cleanliness-across-singapore)
- Water coolers are scarce on popular routes, with the Coney Island trail given as an example (2023) — [The Star](https://thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2023/05/20/beating-the-hot-weather-with-water)
- Inconsistent path markings between pedestrian and cyclist lanes were raised in parliament (Nov 2024). NParks Attention Zones (red dotted lines) require cyclists and PMD users to slow down and give way — [Leader of the Opposition 2024](https://leaderoftheopposition.sg/2024/11/13/consistent-markings-for-segregating-paths-along-park-connector-network); [NParks path types](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharing-the-pcn/top/)
- MOT reported 20 accidents between active-mobility users and pedestrians island-wide from 1 Jul to 31 Aug 2025 — [MOT](https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/collisions-between-cyclists-and-pedestrians-on-cycling-paths-adjacent-to-pedestrian-only-paths/)
- LTA (Sep 2025) tells cyclists to slow down and be ready to dismount in crowded areas — [LTA](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2025/9/media-replies/everyone-plays-a-part-in-creating-safe-environment-on-the-trial.html)

### Inferences
- The app should not assume every PCN segment has a toilet. Rest-stop data should come from mapped toilet and water-cooler points, and malls, hawker centres and petrol stations should be treated as first-class fallbacks.

### Gaps
- There was no data on specific broken water coolers, closed toilet blocks, or shelter shortages at named NParks sites. The NParks/OneService feedback logs are not public.
