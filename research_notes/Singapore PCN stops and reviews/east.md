# Eastern PCN loops (Eastern Coastal Loop, North Eastern Riverine Loop, eastern Round Island Route): attractions, rest stops, reviews, 2026 status

Research date: 2026-11-20. About 16 tool calls. **Coordinates:** none of the sources I fetched gave lat/lon, so every lat/lon cell is blank, as the brief requires. Gaps explains how to fill them.

## Which spots do cyclists and reviewers rate well, and why? (Table A: attractions and stops)

### Takeaway
The best-rated stops are East Coast Lagoon Food Village (cheap, varied, sea breeze), Changi Village (food, the ferry to Ubin, a shady route to Changi Bay PC), Sengkang Riverside Park (big shelter, water) and the Lorong Halus Wetland and red bridge. Coney Island is valued for its rugged gravel trail. Most review evidence comes from blogs and guides. I found few current, cyclist-specific complaint sources.

## Which are mixed, badly reviewed or overrated? (Table B: rest stops for 20+ km rides)

### Takeaway
Water is the weak point. Changi Coastal and ECP have toilets with uneven water-cooler coverage, and at least one reviewer found a cooler not working. ECP and Jalan Kayu suffer from crowding. Mosquitoes are a problem at Sengkang. I found no source that explicitly calls a given eastern hawker centre "overrated."

## Which places are closed, temporarily closed or under redevelopment, and where are the reliable rest points every 5–10 km?

### Takeaway
2026 status changes to flag in the app:
- Seafood Centre / Jumbo: closed 30 Sep 2026.
- Punggol Point Park: closed until about end-2027, toilets open.
- Punggol Container Park: closing Oct–Dec 2026.
- Bedok PC sections: closed until 31 Dec 2026.
- Loyang PC section: closed until Q3 2029.
- Changi Village Hawker Centre: closed 1–15 Oct 2026.

Water is reliable roughly every 5–10 km going clockwise, except along the Changi Coastal stretch.

### Cited Findings
- Bedok PC from Laguna88 to Upper East Coast Rd is closed for Thomson-East Coast Line works until 31 Dec 2026. Bedok PC near 772 Bedok Reservoir and Blk 965D Tampines GreenOpal is closed for bridge works until 31 Dec 2026. Both have diversion routes. — [PCN notices](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/notices)
- The Loyang PC section beside Loyang Ave is closed from 8 Jun 2025 to Q3 2029 for upgrading. — [PCN notices](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/notices)
- Punggol Point Park and its carpark are closed from 11 Aug 2026, with completion expected 31 Dec 2027. Toilets stay open. — [NParks notice (via search snippet; direct fetch 404)](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/6_Jul_2026_Punggol_Point_Park___park_closure_sign__003_.pdf)
- Long Island Phase 1 starts in January 2027. ECP beaches, paths and tracks are reported to stay open. — [littlebigreddot](https://littlebigreddot.com/?p=32906); [URA PR26-50](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-50/)
- The full RIR is 75 km and takes 5–6 h with breaks. The NERL is about 18–26 km, depending on the source. — [sengkangbabies](https://sengkangbabies.com/sports/rir-cycling-map/); [TSL](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/long-distance-cycling-routes-singapore/)
- Clockwise water and toilet sequence: Rower's Bay (T+W) → Sengkang Riverside (T+W) → Punggol Waterfront (T+W) → Lorong Halus (T) → Pasir Ris Park (many T) → Changi Point Ferry (T) → Changi Bay PC (T+W) → Changi Coastal (T, no W) → ECP (many T; W at B1/B2/C1/C3/E2). — [sengkangbabies](https://sengkangbabies.com/sports/rir-cycling-map/); [sportplus](https://sportplus.sg/blogs/news/east-coast-park-the-running-route-every-singapore-runner-starts-with)

### Inferences
- The longest water gap appears to be Changi Bay PC → Changi Coastal → ECP Area G/F. This is an inference from the listed points, not a measured distance. Advise riders to fill up at Changi Bay PC or Changi Village.
- Suggested 5–10 km anchor points: Sengkang Riverside, Punggol Waterway/Waterfront, Pasir Ris Park, Changi Village, Changi Bay PC, ECP Lagoon FV (E2), ECP Parkland Green (C1).

### Gaps
- **Coordinates:** none of the fetched sources gave lat/lon. Fill them from OneMap or the data.gov.sg NParks PCN and parks GeoJSON rather than guessing.
- **Distances between rest points:** no source gave measured segment distances.
- **Source dates:** the sengkangbabies facility list dates from 2022, so the cooler and toilet inventory needs re-verification for 2026.
- **Big Splash:** the closure year is not stated in the snippet and is likely 2019.
- **Seafood Centre vacate date:** one secondary source says October rather than 30 Sep, and this is unconfirmed.
