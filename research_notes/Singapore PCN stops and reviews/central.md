# Singapore Central PCN: attractions, rest stops and reviews (Central Urban Loop, Coast-to-Coast Trail, Rail Corridor, MacRitchie/CCNR edges, city waterfront)

Research date: 2026-11-20. About 22 tool calls in total. Older sources are flagged with their date.

**Coordinate policy.** None of the fetched sources gave lat/lon. The coordinates below are approximate landmark centroids from general geographic knowledge, accurate to roughly 3 decimals (about 100 m). They are marked `~` and must be checked against OneMap or OSM before shipping in the JSON overlay. Where I was not confident, the coordinate is blank.

## Which spots do cyclists and reviewers rate well, and why?

### Takeaway
The best-rated central stops are the big parks with full facilities: Bishan-AMK Park, Jurong Lake Gardens, Lorong Halus Wetland, Punggol Point and Coney Island. The heritage Rail Corridor (Bukit Timah Railway Station, Hillview/Rail Mall) and Marina Barrage are also well rated. Reviewers praise space, views, shade/nature, and bike-specific amenities such as repair stations, racks and water coolers.

### Cited Findings
- Bishan-AMK Park: 62 ha, one of the largest central urban parks, "popular choice for cycling, jogging and family gatherings". A Trip.com reviewer calls it "a good place to cycle around". — [NParks Bishan-AMK](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/bishan-ang-mo-kio-park); [Trip.com review](https://au.trip.com/travel-guide/review-attraction/bishan-ang-mo-kio-park-31691614/154834869/)
- NParks Central Urban Loop brochure: a "wide and spacious track" along the connector links Bishan-AMK Park and Kallang Riverside Park. — [NParks Central Urban Loop PDF](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/-/media/nparks-real-content/gardens-parks-and-nature/park-connector-network/brochures/central-urban-loop.pdf)
- GoCycling AMK bike rental is about 500 m from the eastern edge of Bishan-AMK Park. From there riders can follow the Kallang River toward Marina Bay. — [Little Day Out](https://www.littledayout.com/rent-a-bike-to-cycle-at-bishan-ang-mo-kio-park-gocycling-ang-mo-kio/)
- Coast-to-Coast (C2C) Trail: 36 km from Jurong Lake Gardens to Coney Island Park, with 10 checkpoints, 18 parks/nature areas and 11 park connectors. Checkpoints include Jurong Lake Gardens, Bukit Batok Nature Park, Hindhede Drive, Adam Road and Bishan-AMK Park (the list in search snippets was truncated). — [NParks C2C](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/c2c); [C2C Trail Guide PDF (Dec 2018)](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/C2C_Trail_Guide_181224.pdf); [MustShareNews (2019)](https://mustsharenews.com/coast-to-coast-trail/)
- Jurong Lake Gardens (C2C start) has eateries (Burp Kitchen & Bar, Canopy Garden Dining, McDonald's), shelters, vending machines, a DIY bicycle repair station and toilets. — [Time Out C2C guide, via search snippet](https://timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/the-ultimate-guide-to-the-coast-to-coast-trail). The full page returned 403, so this is unverified beyond the snippet.
- Jurong Lake Gardens is now complete with the rejuvenated Chinese and Japanese Gardens (circa Sept 2024). Reviewers describe "miles of cycling and jogging paths". — [NParks news](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/news/news-detail/nparks-completes-jurong-lake-gardens-with-opening-of-rejuvenated-chinese-and-japanese-gardens); [NParks cycling in JLG](https://lightsbythelake.nparks.gov.sg/cycling-in-jurong-lake-gardens/)
- Rower's Bay, on the final C2C stretch near Coney Island, has a boardwalk, wetland, shelters, a bicycle repair station and toilets (2019 report). — [Time Out / ST snippet via search](https://timeout.com/singapore/news/do-you-have-what-it-takes-to-conquer-the-36km-coast-to-coast-trail-021620)
- Lorong Halus Wetland is described as "a great mid-journey pitstop" with a washroom, sheltered pavilion and a water cooler for bottle refills. — [TheSmartLocal long-distance routes](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/long-distance-cycling-routes-singapore/amp/)
- Coney Island Park: 81 ha, open 7am–7pm, "scenic cycling paths", bicycle parking at both entrances. Bikes can be rented at Punggol Point Park. — [NParks Coney Island](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/coney-island-park); [Little Day Out](https://www.littledayout.com/ten-things-you-need-to-know-before-visiting-coney-island-park/)
- Punggol Point Park has restrooms. This is the recommended toilet stop before riding Coney Island. — [NParks Punggol Point](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/punggol-point-park); [Little Day Out](https://www.littledayout.com/punggol-point-park-much-more-than-a-seaside-stroll/)
- Bukit Timah Railway Station reopened in 2022 after restoration, with a heritage gallery. Time Out suggests starting rides there and notes a "quaint cafe" for refreshment. — [Little Day Out Rail Corridor guide](https://www.littledayout.com/rail-corridor-guide-sights-side-trips-stops/); [Time Out best cycling trails](https://WWW.TIMEOUT.PT/singapore/things-to-do/the-best-cycling-trails-in-singapore)
- The Rail Mall (Hillview) brands itself bike-friendly with secure bicycle parking. The rebuilt Hillview bridge gives direct Rail Corridor access to The Rail Mall's food and water. — [The Rail Mall](https://railmall.com.sg/?p=11); [NParks RC North opening](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/news/news-detail/rail-corridor-(north)-opens-to-public--offering-over-21-km-of-north-south-connectivity-and-greater-accessibility-with-more-than-30-access-paths)
- Marina Barrage: reviewers praise the 360° skyline view, picnics and kite flying. "Enjoyed cycling day with family ~ Love the view so much". — [Trip.com Marina Barrage](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/singapore/marina-barrage-22847623); [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/410266)
- Kallang Riverside Park: "serene and beautiful park along the Kallang River" for cycling. Its PCN path under Nicoll Highway connects to Marina Bay Sands. A Komoot tip (2021) praises the Kallang Basin sunset. — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/410266); [Little Day Out Marina/Kallang route](https://www.littledayout.com/cycling-around-marina-bay-kallang-basin-route-map-landmarks/); [Komoot (2021)](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/3240520)
- LTA recommended cycling food spots (May 2025): **Amoy Street Food Centre** (six Michelin-distinguished stalls), at the end of a Jurong East → Ulu Pandan PCN → Rail Corridor/Greenway → Telok Ayer route. **ChikSoo Korean Kitchen** at Oasis Terraces, Punggol, at the end of a Singapore River → Rochor → Kallang River → Bishan Park → Punggol Waterway route. — [LTA Connect](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/who_we_are/statistics_and_publications/Connect/recommended-cycling-food-spots.html)
- Bidadari Park: 13 ha, supports cycling along sheltered linkways, toilets on site. One blogger found it "maybe a little small for cycling". — [EdgeProp](https://www.edgeprop.sg/property-news/bidadari-park-award-winning-urban-landscape-and-biophilic-sanctuary); [abbatravel blog (Sep 2024)](https://abbatravel.blogspot.com/2024/09/singapore-to-do-08sep24-bidadari-park.html)

### Inferences
- The highest-value POIs for the overlay are places that combine toilets, water and shelter with bike racks or a repair station: JLG, Rower's Bay, Lorong Halus, Punggol Point and Bishan-AMK.
- Food reviews are about destinations (hawker centres, malls) rather than PCN-side kiosks. Riders typically detour about 200–500 m to malls or hawkers.

### Gaps
- No Reddit r/singapore or r/singaporecycling threads surfaced in the searches. Cyclist sentiment here comes mainly from blogs, Trip.com/Wanderlog snippets and official sources.
- I found no review data for cafés inside Bishan-AMK Park or Bidadari. One family review says Bidadari has no restaurants ([Sassymama](https://www.sassymamasg.com/play-favourite-parks-in-singapore/)).
- No toilet-specific info was found for Bishan-AMK Park, though large NParks parks normally have them. Verify on the NParks map.
- The full C2C 10-checkpoint list could not be retrieved; the Time Out page returned 403. The NParks C2C PDF is the authoritative source.

## Which are mixed, badly reviewed or overrated?

### Takeaway
Most negatives are about crowding and rules, not the places themselves. Marina Barrage and Kallang Riverside get crowded on weekends. Gardens by the Bay is effectively off-limits to riding inside, and its waterfront promenade has been closed to cyclists since May 2026. Coney Island's facilities are thin and inconsistently reported. Bidadari is small for riding. The Rail Corridor has unlit stretches, uneven surfaces and bridges where riders must dismount. The once cyclist-favourite Wheeler's Yard café is permanently closed.

### Cited Findings
- Kallang Riverside Park: "Sunday very crowded". Another reviewer warns "the road is busy especially during evening time". — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/410266)
- Marina Barrage: "quite crowded during weekends. Be sure to come early if want a nice spot for picnic." — [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/singapore/marina-barrage-22847623)
- Gardens by the Bay: bicycles, scooters and skates are "prohibited throughout the Gardens for safety and crowd control" (secondary source, a family blog). The Waterfront Promenade has had no cyclist access since 4 May 2026 for the Bay South–Bay East bridge works. Cyclists heading to Marina Barrage must detour around the Bay South perimeter, while pedestrians keep access with a small diversion. — [Little Steps Asia](https://www.littlestepsasia.com/singapore/play/outdoor-activities/cycling-gardens-by-the-bay-singapore/); [Little Big Red Dot (2026)](https://littlebigreddot.com/gardens-by-the-bay-cycling-diversion-4-may-2026/); [Gardens by the Bay plan your visit](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/plan-your-visit.html)
- Gardens by the Bay also had no cycling on the Waterfront Promenade on 25–26 Apr 2026 (Singapore T100) and a traffic advisory for OCBC Cycle on 10 May 2026. — [GBTB notice](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/learn-with-us/explore-resources/videos/how-to-make-a-hanging-kokedama.html); [GBTB](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/plan-your-visit.html)
- Coney Island toilet reports conflict. City Nomads says the only toilets are at the East Entrance; Little Day Out says portable toilets near Beach Area E; Singapore Spirit says there are no toilets or food at all. — [City Nomads](https://citynomads.com/?p=221721); [Little Day Out](https://www.littledayout.com/ten-things-you-need-to-know-before-visiting-coney-island-park/); [Singapore Spirit](https://www.singapore-spirit.com/guides/coney-island-guide/)
- Bidadari is "maybe a little small for cycling". — [abbatravel (Sep 2024)](https://abbatravel.blogspot.com/2024/09/singapore-to-do-08sep24-bidadari-park.html)
- Rail Corridor: cyclists must dismount and push across truss bridges. The corridor is unlit at night apart from access points and Bukit Timah Railway Station (ST via Tabla). NParks advisory of 26 May 2026: some stretches may have uneven surfaces. — [Tabla/ST](https://www.tabla.com.sg/news/singapore/A-E28098rail-lyE28099-enjoyable-walkabout-18256551); [Rail Corridor news](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/latest-news)
- Rail Corridor has no on-trail toilets at key nodes. At the Buona Vista community node, the nearest toilets are The Metropolis and Buona Vista MRT. At Hillview, they are Hillview MRT and 9 Mile Platform. — [NParks Buona Vista node](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/buona-vista-community-node/); [NParks RC North](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/news/news-detail/rail-corridor-(north)-opens-to-public--offering-over-21-km-of-north-south-connectivity-and-greater-accessibility-with-more-than-30-access-paths)
- Wheeler's Yard (28 Lorong Ampas, Balestier), the bicycle-themed café "favourite among cyclists", closed permanently on 30 Sep 2023. Its successor, Rounding, was ordered shut after 10 Mar 2024. Many listicles still list it, so it should be **excluded or marked closed**. — [Mothership (Sep 2023)](https://mothership.sg/2023/09/wheelers-yard-closing); [Mothership (Feb 2024)](https://mothership.sg/2024/02/rounding-cafe-to-close/); stale listing: [HungryGoWhere](https://hungrygowhere.com/what-to-eat/cafe-pit-stops-for-cyclists)
- MacRitchie / Central Catchment Nature Reserve: cycling is not allowed. NParks lists CCNR alongside Sungei Buloh as areas where cycling is "strictly not allowed". — [NParks dos and don'ts](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/when-visiting-parks/etiquette/nature-parks-reserve-dos-donts); [NParks PCN sharing](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/sharingpcn/)
- Mountain biking is legal only on designated trails, such as Bukit Timah Nature Reserve's MTB trail, Chestnut Nature Park (8.2 km MTB loop), Kent Ridge and Ketam. A June 2025 report notes cyclists straying into the live-firing zones next to Chestnut. — [Malay Mail (Jun 2025)](https://www.malaymail.com/news/singapore/2025/06/18/bullet-injury-highlights-risks-of-ignoring-live-firing-signs-at-chestnut-park-in-singapore-as-cyclists-continue-to-enter-restricted-zones/180792); [TheSmartLocal Chestnut](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/chestnut-nature-park/amp)
- Jurong Lake Gardens has designated "No Cycling Zones". Riders must dismount there and in crowded event areas. — [NParks cycling in JLG](https://lightsbythelake.nparks.gov.sg/cycling-in-jurong-lake-gardens/)

### Inferences
- Sentiment "mixed" fits Marina Barrage and Kallang Riverside (great views, weekend crowds), Coney Island (lovely but facilities uncertain) and Gardens by the Bay (an iconic POI that is not rideable).
- Explicitly "bad" reviews of specific PCN stops were not found. The negatives are about crowding and access rather than quality.

### Gaps
- I found no TripAdvisor or Google review aggregates with numeric ratings for individual PCN stops.
- The Singapore River promenade has no explicit dismount rule for Boat Quay or Clarke Quay. URA describes a 15 m promenade with a separate cycling path ([URA](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/urban-design/singapore-river/)), and general advice is to slow down or dismount in crowds. Nightlife crowding is likely but not sourced.

## Which places are closed, temporarily closed or under redevelopment as of 2026?

### Takeaway
Four closures or limits matter as of late 2026. (1) Rail Corridor (South), Henderson Rd to Spooner Rd, has been closed since March 2026 for Phase 2 trail works. Completion is expected 31 July 2027, and cycling is banned in the closed section. (2) The Gardens by the Bay Waterfront Promenade has been closed to cyclists since 4 May 2026, with no reopening notice found. (3) A Rail Corridor (North) diversion runs between Choa Chu Kang Rd and Stagmont Ring Rd; its "completion by 2026" status is unconfirmed. (4) Wheeler's Yard is permanently closed (2023).

### Cited Findings
- Rail Corridor (South) Phase 1, Jalan Kilang Barat to Henderson Road, **reopened on 2 Mar 2026**, ahead of the 31 Jul 2026 target. — [Rail Corridor closure notice Feb 2026](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/latest-news/important-notices/closure-notice-feb); [Rail Corridor latest news](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/latest-news)
- Rail Corridor (South) Phase 2, after Henderson Rd to before Spooner Rd, is **closed**. Sources give the start as 2 Mar 2026 or 9 Mar 2026 (they conflict). Expected completion is 31 Jul 2027. Walking, jogging and cycling are not allowed, and the signed alternative is along Jalan Bukit Merah. — [Closure notice PDF (Feb 2026)](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/files/RC%20Notices/RC_South_trail_closure_signs___Feb_2026__Approved_.pdf); [Rail Corridor homepage](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg)
- Rail Corridor (North) is diverted between Choa Chu Kang Road and Stagmont Ring Road for enhancement works, "expected to be completed by 2026". The diversion notice is dated 9 Feb 2023. — [Diversion notice](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/latest-news/important-notices/permalink)
- Rail Corridor trail advisory (26 May 2026): some stretches have uneven surfaces. — [Rail Corridor latest news](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/latest-news)
- Gardens by the Bay Waterfront Promenade: no cyclist access from 4 May 2026 for the Bay South–Bay East bridge construction (Q2 2026). The Children's Garden promenade entrance has been closed since 26 Mar 2026. — [Little Big Red Dot](https://littlebigreddot.com/gardens-by-the-bay-cycling-diversion-4-may-2026/); [GBTB promotions page](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/promotions)
- Jurong Lake Gardens works are finished: the Chinese and Japanese Gardens reopened (~2024), and an earlier 2023–24 Jurong PC closure has lapsed. — [NParks](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/news/news-detail/nparks-completes-jurong-lake-gardens-with-opening-of-rejuvenated-chinese-and-japanese-gardens); [PCN closure notice (Mar 2023)](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/31_Mar_2023_PCN_Closure_Notice_Jurong_PC_end_March_24.pdf)
- LTA's AMK/Bishan/Toa Payoh cycling path programme: another 16 km of cycling paths were "slated for completion in 2026" (stated Oct 2022). — [LTA Oct 2022](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2022/10/news-releases/improved-active-mobility-connectivity-with-new-cycling-paths.html)
- Buona Vista community node opened April 2024 (fitness area, playground, lawn). Water and power points were slated "by May" 2024, but installation is unconfirmed. — [NParks node page](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/buona-vista-community-node/)
- Wheeler's Yard closed permanently on 30 Sep 2023. — [Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2023/09/wheelers-yard-closing)

### Inferences
- Rides that go from the Rail Corridor south to Tanjong Pagar or the Greenway (such as LTA's Amoy Street route) must reroute via Jalan Bukit Merah until mid-2027.
- The GBTB promenade closure is likely to persist into 2027, as bridge works seldom finish in six months. This is unconfirmed and should be shown as "closed to cyclists, check GBTB site".

### Gaps
- I found no current (post-May 2026) GBTB notice confirming whether the promenade has reopened to cyclists.
- I could not confirm whether the RC North Choa Chu Kang–Stagmont diversion has ended.
- I found no 2026 closure info for the Kallang PCN, Bidadari, Toa Payoh or Potong Pasir.

## Where are the reliable rest points roughly every 5–10 km?

### Takeaway
- **C2C (36 km):** reliable full-service stops at Jurong Lake Gardens (km 0), Bukit Batok Nature Park, Hindhede / Bukit Timah Nature Reserve visitor area, Adam Road (hawker centre), Bishan-AMK Park (about midway), then the Punggol/Lorong Halus/Rower's Bay end. Ten checkpoints over 36 km gives an average spacing of about 3.6 km.
- **Rail Corridor:** has no on-trail toilets. Riders rely on adjacent nodes: Hillview MRT/Rail Mall, 9 Mile Platform, Bukit Timah Railway Station and Buona Vista MRT/The Metropolis.
- **Central loop / waterfront:** Bishan-AMK Park → Kallang Riverside → Marina Barrage → Bay East / Gardens by the Bay (perimeter) is a sequence of public facilities roughly 4–6 km apart.

### Cited Findings
- C2C: 10 checkpoints over 36 km. — [NParks C2C](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/c2c); [C2C guide PDF](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/files/C2C_Trail_Guide_181224.pdf)
- JLG has toilets, a DIY bike repair station, vending machines and food. — [Time Out snippet](https://timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/the-ultimate-guide-to-the-coast-to-coast-trail)
- Rower's Bay has toilets, shelters and a bike repair station (2019). Lorong Halus has a washroom, pavilion and water cooler. Punggol Point Park has restrooms. — sources above.
- Rail Corridor toilets: Hillview MRT, 9 Mile Platform, The Metropolis and Buona Vista MRT. — [NParks node page](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/buona-vista-community-node/); [NParks RC North](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/news/news-detail/rail-corridor-(north)-opens-to-public--offering-over-21-km-of-north-south-connectivity-and-greater-accessibility-with-more-than-30-access-paths)
- Marina Barrage is about 2.3 km by bike from the Bay East Garden visitor centre (older article). — [TheSmartLocal Bay East](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bay-east-garden/amp)

### Inferences
- The longest gap without guaranteed toilets on these routes is the Rail Corridor itself: about 5–6 km between Hillview and Bukit Timah Railway Station/Buona Vista without trail-side toilets. The overlay should surface the off-trail MRT and mall toilets.
- Riders should not count on toilets on Coney Island. Use Punggol Point Park (west) or Lorong Halus (east).

### Gaps
- No official water-point dataset was found. NParks parks usually have drinking fountains, but locations were not sourced. The NParks PCN map (pcn.nparks.gov.sg) is the likely source.
