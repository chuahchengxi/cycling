# Bike rental shops and kiosks near Singapore's park connectors and tourist areas

All sources checked 2026-11-20 unless noted. Prices in SGD. "Not found" = not stated in any source consulted. Coordinates intentionally omitted; postcodes given only where a source states them.

Source age key: GoCycling official site (live, checked 2026-11-20); TheMoneyBees (prices "as of October 2026", update note 13 Oct 2026); TheSmartLocal (last updated 18 Nov 2025); Little Day Out ECP guide (30 Mar 2024); Little Day Out Ubin guide (30 Sep 2021); Little Day Out Pasir Ris guide (2017, checked Jul 2018).

## Which rental shops are confirmed open in 2025–2026 (and which are closed or uncertain)?

### Takeaway
GoCycling is the only chain whose outlets I could confirm on its own live site (8 named outlets, Nov 2026). Other shops (Coastline Leisure / Bike Stop, Punggol independents, Pulau Ubin shops, GoGreen Sentosa) are confirmed only by third-party guides from 2024 to 2026, and some of those guides contradict each other on whether Coastline's East Coast outlets still exist.

### Cited Findings

**GoCycling, network-wide terms (official site)**
- Prices: adult single from $10/hr, kids from $8/hr, child-seat bike from $16/hr, tandem from $20/hr, family bike from $40/hr (only at ECP C4, Changi Beach C1, West Coast Park, Pasir Ris Park), pedal go-karts from $13/30 min (Changi Beach C1, West Coast Park). Night rental (6pm to before opening) adult from $20 plus a compulsory $2 lock — [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/)
- Promo: free 2nd hour on weekdays, free 3rd hour on weekends, if you start before 5pm and pay upfront — [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/)
- Return: you can rent and return at any full-service outlet. Self-service outlets are standalone, so you return to the same kiosk. $50 cash deposit. Last registration and latest hourly return are 30 min before closing — [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/)
- E-bikes and helmets are not mentioned on the official page — [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/)
- The site says "11 outlets islandwide" but its text lists only 8. The 3 missing ones are probably Marina Bay, Punggol and Jurong Lake Gardens, which third-party guides list, but the map is an image so I could not confirm this — [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/)

### Closures and conflicts to flag
- **Coastline Leisure / Bike Stop at ECP Area B1 and E2: conflicting status.** One search-result summary of a 2026 guide said Coastline's B1 and E2 locations are "believed to be no longer available", while the Marine Parade Central outlet remains ([LDO via search](https://www.littledayout.com/east-coast-park-bicycle-rental/)). However, TheMoneyBees (prices as of Oct 2026) and TheSmartLocal (Nov 2025) still list both with hours. The LDO page I fetched (dated 30 Mar 2024) did not contain that closure line. Treat both as unverified and phone before going — [TheMoneyBees](https://themoneybees.co/blog/bicycle-rental-singapore); [TSL](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bicycle-rental-singapore/)
- **Coastline Leisure at Pasir Ris Park Carpark C** appears in a 2016 NParks article but a later Little Day Out guide reports it closed — [NParks Buzz 2016](https://nparks.gov.sg/nparksbuzz/issue-28-vol-1-2016/highlights/new-treats-to-enjoy-new-places-to-explore); [LDO Pasir Ris](https://www.littledayout.com/pasir-ris-bike-rental-guide/)
- **Lifestyle Bike N Skate pitstops (ECP):** a source could not confirm they still exist — [search summary, themoneybees/LDO](https://themoneybees.co/blog/bicycle-rental-singapore)
- **GoCycling ECP G1 hours conflict:** official 10am–7pm vs TSL 8am–10pm. Trust the official site — [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/)
- **East Coast Seafood Centre redevelopment** (NParks landlord; Jumbo closed, Long Beach moved to Coastal PlayGrove) may affect nearby ECP facilities. No bike-shop closure was linked to it in the sources — [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/news/east-coast-park-seafood-centre-up-for-a-revamp-jumbo-seafood-closes-while-long-beach-relocates-060926)

### Inferences
- For an app, GoCycling full-service outlets (ECP C4, Pasir Ris Park, Changi Beach C1, Changi T2, West Coast Park) are the safest "confirmed open, can return elsewhere" set. That makes one-way rides possible, e.g. Changi T2 to ECP C4, subject to space at the outlet.
- Shops listed only by blogs should be shown with a "last verified" date and an "unverified" badge.

### Gaps
- None of the sources give a postcode for GoGreen Sentosa or the Pulau Ubin shops. Pekan Ubin shops use house numbers (e.g. No. 25), not 6-digit postcodes.
- I found no official confirmation that GoCycling's Marina Bay, Punggol Jetty, Punggol Park or Jurong Lake Gardens outlets are still open in Nov 2026 (the official page lists 8 of 11 outlets in text).
- E-bike rentals: no staffed shop in these sources offers e-bikes. I did not search specifically for e-bike rental.
- No Google business listings were checked directly, so "permanently closed" flags from Google were not verified.

## Which areas have no staffed rental and rely on shared bikes only?

### Takeaway
No source explicitly lists "shared-bike-only" areas. From the shop list, Gardens by the Bay itself, Changi Village proper and the town-park kiosks (ECP G1, Changi Beach C7, Pasir Ris Town Park) have no staffed counter, and riders there rely on self-service kiosks or Anywheel / HelloRide.

### Cited Findings
- Cycling is generally not allowed inside most of Gardens by the Bay. Cyclists park at bike stands at Bay South and walk. The nearest staffed rental is GoCycling @ Singapore Flyer, facing the Gardens — [Little Steps Asia](https://www.littlestepsasia.com/singapore/play/outdoor-activities/cycling-gardens-by-the-bay-singapore/); [LDO GoCycling Marina Bay](https://www.littledayout.com/gocycling-marina-bay-bike-rental-singapore-flyer-get-pedalling/)
- Self-service (unstaffed) GoCycling kiosks: ECP G1, Changi Beach Park C7 (3 Changi Ferry Rd, near Changi Village) and Pasir Ris Town Park. For C7, you call the number on site to rent — [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/); [TSL](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bicycle-rental-singapore/)
- Anywheel (app-based, from $1 per 30 min for a regular bike) and HelloRide are islandwide bike-share services, not rental shops — [TheMoneyBees](https://themoneybees.co/blog/bicycle-rental-singapore); [TSL](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bicycle-rental-singapore/)

### Inferences
- Changi Village: no staffed shop was found in the village itself. The closest are the self-service C7 kiosk and the staffed C1 outlet at 260 Nicoll Drive.
- Areas with no rental found in any source: the Southern Ridges, Bishan-AMK Park, Rail Corridor, Mandai and the Botanic Gardens. Riders there likely rely on shared bikes, but I did not research this.

### Gaps
- No source maps shared-bike availability or bans by area (e.g. bike-share parking rules on Sentosa or at Gardens by the Bay).
- No official Gardens by the Bay policy page was retrieved. The cycling ban comes from a blog.

## Any official guided bike tours for tourists?

### Takeaway
Several commercial guided tours run in 2026 (city heritage, Marina Bay night ride, Pulau Ubin). They are sold through Go City, Klook, Pelago and others. I could not confirm STB licensing for any of them.

### Cited Findings
- **Let's Go Bike Singapore, Historical Singapore Bicycle Tour:** 4 hrs, starts 8:30am, pre-booking required, flat to gently rolling terrain; on the Go City pass — [Go City](https://gocity.com/singapore/en-us/attractions/lets-go-bike-singapore)
- **Lion City Bike Tour:** from USD 67.06; includes bike, guide, poncho, refreshments, helmet on request; meets at Nicoll Highway MRT — [Pelago](https://www.pelago.com/en-US/activity/p1ckw-lion-city-bike-tour-singapore/)
- **4-hour Singapore cycling tour (808 Tours):** US$77.99/pax; starts near Bukit Timah Railway Station; dates from Aug 2026 — [Klook](https://www.klook.com/activity/146857-4-hours-singapore-cycling-tour/?aid=17620)
- **Marina Bay evening bike tour** — [Luxury Escapes / Klook](https://luxuryescapes.com/sg/experience/singapore-marina-bay-evening-bike-tour/klo:34587)
- **Pulau Ubin tours:** Asian Detours 4-hr "Ubin Bike Trail" meets at 34 Pulau Ubin, 1 min from the jetty; ferry usually excluded; about $95–97/adult — [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com.au/things-to-do/ubin-bike-trail-adventure.a51892497.activity-details); [Asian Trails](https://www.asiantrails.travel/trail/half-day-ubin-bike-trail/). A Pelago Ubin tour from USD 68.44 meets at Changi Point Ferry Terminal and includes the two-way ferry and bike; child-seat bike free on request — [Pelago](https://www.pelago.com/en/activity/pg1ue-pulau-ubin-bike-tour-singapore)
- **Sentosa:** GoGreen guided Segway rides of 30/60 min with training (Segway, not bike) — [Headout](https://www.headout.com/singapore-bike-tours/go-green-segway-eco-adventure-sentosa-e-7407)
- A list of 10 bike tours (updated Jan 2026) includes a Marina Bay night ride, a Pulau Ubin half-day and a bike-and-food tour — [Cycloscope](https://cycloscope.net/10-best-bike-tours-in-singapore-cycling-vacations-in-the-lion-city/)

### Inferences
- These are commercial operators sold via OTAs. None was shown to be an "official" STB product.

### Gaps
- I did not verify STB tourist-guide licensing or VisitSingapore listings for these operators (VisitSingapore was not fetched).
- No official NParks-run guided ride was found.
- Let's Go Bike's own site, meeting point and current price were not found.
